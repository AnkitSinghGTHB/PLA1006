# Partnership - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./01_Arithmetic_Percentages_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Partnership.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The `Investment * Time` Rule:** Profit share is ALWAYS the ratio of the product of `Investment Amount * Number of Months`. Forget about writing massive equations, just write the timeline weights.

---

### Question 1
**Question:** A and B invest in ratio 3:2. 5% goes to charity. A's share is Rs. 855. B's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 570  B) 580  C) 590  D) 600]`
*   **Mental Trick (Ignore the Distraction):**
    *   The "5% to charity" is a distractor! It affects the *total* profit, but it doesn't change the 3:2 ratio in which A and B split the remaining money.
    *   Since A gets 3 parts and B gets 2 parts:
    *   `3 parts = 855`.
    *   `1 part = 855 / 3 = 285`.
    *   B's share = `2 parts = 285 * 2 = 570`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 570**.

### Question 2
**Question:** A, B, C enter in ratio 3:3:4. After 4 months, A increases share by 50%. Profit is 1100. B's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 300  B) 360  C) 240  D) 320]`
*   **Mental Trick (Weighted Ratio):**
    *   A invests 3 units for 4 months. Then increases by 50% (becomes 4.5 units) for the remaining 8 months.
    *   `A_weight = (3 * 4) + (4.5 * 8) = 12 + 36 = 48`.
    *   `B_weight = (3 * 12) = 36`.
    *   `C_weight = (4 * 12) = 48`.
    *   Ratio A:B:C = `48 : 36 : 48` = `4 : 3 : 4`.
    *   Total parts = `4 + 3 + 4 = 11 parts`.
    *   `11 parts = 1100`  →  `1 part = 100`.
    *   B's share = `3 parts = 300`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 300**.

### Question 3
**Question:** A=6500 for 6m, B=8400 for 5m, C=10000 for 3m. A receives 5% off the top of 7400 profit for working. B's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1900  B) 2660  C) 2800  D) 2840]`
*   **Mental Trick (Peel the Top):**
    *   First, A takes his 5% salary. `10% of 7400 = 740`. So `5% = 370`.
    *   Remaining profit to split = `7400 - 370 = 7030`.
    *   Now find ratios (`Amount * Months`):
    *   A: `6500 * 6 = 39000`
    *   B: `8400 * 5 = 42000`
    *   C: `10000 * 3 = 30000`
    *   Ratio A:B:C = `39 : 42 : 30` = `13 : 14 : 10`.
    *   Total parts = `13 + 14 + 10 = 37 parts`.
    *   `37 parts = 7030`. Since 37 * 2 = 74, ``1 part = 190``.
    *   B gets 14 parts. `14 * 190 = 2660`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 2660**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Varun invests 1 lakh for 12 months. Vikram joins with 50,000 for 6 months limit. Total profit 50,000. Varun's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10,000  B) 20,000  C) 40,000  D) 30,000]`
*   **Mental Trick (Scaling zeroes):**
    *   Drop the thousands. Varun = `100 * 12 = 1200`.
    *   Vikram = `50 * 6 = 300`.
    *   Ratio Varun:Vikram = `1200 : 300` = `4 : 1`.
    *   Total 5 parts. Varun takes 4 of them.
    *   `4/5 of 50,000 = 40,000`.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 40,000**.

### Question 10
**Question:** A begins with Rs. 85,000. B joins later with Rs. 42,500. Profit at end of year divided 3:1. How long did B join?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4 months  B) 5 months  C) 6 months  D) 8 months]`
*   **Mental Trick (Relative Base):**
    *   Instantly notice that B's investment (42,500) is exactly **half** of A's (85,000).
    *   Let A's money be `2` and B's money be `1`.
    *   A was there for 12 months. Weight = `2 * 12 = 24`.
    *   Let B's months be `x`. Weight = `1 * x = x`.
    *   Profit ratio is given as 3:1. So, `24 / x = 3 / 1`.
    *   `3x = 24  →  x = 8`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 8 months**.
