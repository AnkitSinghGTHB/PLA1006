### Question 1
**Question:** Avg weight of 25 students = 48 kg. Teacher included, avg increases by 2 kg. Teacher's weight?
*   **Options:** `[A) 100  B) 102  C) 110  D) 115]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Teacher added `2 kg` to each of the 26 people's average. Extra weight = `26 × 2 = 52`. Plus the base avg he "uses up" = 48. Teacher = `48 + 52 = 100`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 100 kg**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Avg of 10 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one weighing 70 kg. New person's weight?
*   **Options:** `[A) 75  B) 80  C) 95  D) 100]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Increase of 2.5 across 10 people = `2.5 × 10 = 25 kg` extra. New person = `70 + 25 = 95 kg`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 95 kg**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Bowling avg = 14.6. Takes 5 wickets for 25 runs, avg drops by 0.6. Wickets taken before this match?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 75  C) 100  D) 110]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let old wickets = n. Old total runs = `14.6n`. New: `(14.6n + 25)/(n + 5) = 14.0`.
*   `14.6n + 25 = 14n + 70` → `0.6n = 45` → `n = 75`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 75**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Avg of 27 results = 19. Avg of first 13 = 15. Avg of last 13 = 19. Find 14th result.
*   **Options:** `[A) 32  B) 53  C) 68  D) 71]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total = `27 × 19 = 513`. First 13 = `13 × 15 = 195`. Last 13 = `13 × 19 = 247`.
*   14th is counted in neither or both groups. Since first 13 + last 13 = 26, and 14th is the overlap:
*   `195 + 247 - 14th = 513` → `14th = 442 - 513`? No: `195 + 247 = 442`. But total 27 results sum to 513. The 14th is shared between groups: `14th = 195 + 247 - 513 = -71`? 
*   Actually, first 13 (results 1-13) and last 13 (results 15-27) DON'T include the 14th!
*   `14th = 513 - 195 - 247 = 71`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 71**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Avg of 60 numbers = 45. Remove numbers 59 and 60. New avg of remaining 58?
*   **Options:** `[A) 44.5  B) 45  C) 47  D) 47.5]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total = `60 × 45 = 2700`. Remove `59 + 60 = 119`. New sum = `2700 - 119 = 2581`.
*   New avg = `2581/58`. Quick: `58 × 44 = 2552`. `2581 - 2552 = 29`. `29/58 = 0.5`. Avg = `44.5`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 44.5**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Avg salary of 25 employees = Rs. 6000. With manager, avg becomes 6400. Manager's salary?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs. 15,000  B) Rs. 15,500  C) Rs. 16,400  D) Rs. 17,200]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Manager boosted avg by 400 for 26 people. Extra = `26 × 400 = 10,400`. Plus base = 6000. Manager = `6000 + 10,400 = 16,400`.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 16,400**.

### Question 7
**Question:** Avg age of Mr. & Mrs. Arun 4 years ago = 32. Present avg of Mr., Mrs. & son = 25. Son's age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 6]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Current sum of couple = `(32 + 4) × 2 = 72`. Current sum of trio = `25 × 3 = 75`.
*   Son = `75 - 72 = 3`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 3**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Mark entered 60 instead of 30. Avg increased by 1/3. Number of pupils?
*   **Options:** `[A) 75  B) 84  C) 90  D) 95]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Error = `60 - 30 = 30`. Spread = `1/3` per student. Students = `30 ÷ (1/3) = 90`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 90**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Avg height of 40 boys = 170 cm. Remaining 20 boys avg = 164 cm. Avg of class of 60?
*   **Options:** `[A) 152  B) 168  C) 175  D) 190]`
*   **Mental Trick (Weighted Avg):** Ratio 40:20 = 2:1. Use base 164. Deviation of group 1 = +6.
*   Weighted: `6 × 2/3 = 4`. Avg = `164 + 4 = 168`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 168 cm**.

### Question 10
**Question:** First number is twice the second and thrice the third. Avg of three = 275. Difference between first and third?
*   **Options:** `[A) 175  B) 225  C) 278  D) 300]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let third = x. First = 3x. Second = (first/2) = 3x/2.
*   Sum = `3x + 3x/2 + x = 3x + 1.5x + x = 5.5x`. Avg = `5.5x/3 = 275` → `x = 275 × 3 / 5.5 = 825/5.5 = 150`.
*   First = `3 × 150 = 450`. Difference = `450 - 150 = 300`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 300**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Avg weight of 58 students was 41 kg. One was 36 but entered as 38, another was 48 but entered as 43. Actual avg?
*   **Options:** `[A) 41.05  B) 43.65  C) 58.73  D) Cannot be determined]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Error 1: entered 38 instead of 36 → `+2 extra`. Error 2: entered 43 instead of 48 → `-5 short`. Net error = `+2 - 5 = -3`.
*   Actual total = `Wrong total + 3 = 58 × 41 + 3 = 2378 + 3 = 2381`.
*   Actual avg = `2381/58 = 41.05`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 41.05 kgs**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Avg of 12 men decreases by 2 kg when one weighing 91 kg is replaced. New man's weight?
*   **Options:** `[A) 85  B) 95  C) 65  D) 67]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Decrease of 2 across 12 people = `12 × 2 = 24 kg` lost. New person = `91 - 24 = 67 kg`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 67 kg**.

### Question 13
**Question:** Avg of 20 sailors increases by 1.2 kg when one weighing 50 kg is replaced. New recruit's weight?
*   **Options:** `[A) 68  B) 70  C) 74  D) 72]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Increase of 1.2 across 20 = `24 kg` extra. New = `50 + 24 = 74 kg`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 74 kg**.

### Question 14
**Question:** Two mixtures mixed 5:3. Mix 1 has N:H:O = 4:5:1. Mix 2 has 5:7:3. Need 30% nitrogen. If not 30%, dilute with oxygen. Ratio of original mixture to added oxygen?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4:2  B) 4:4  C) 4:1  D) 4:4]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Mix 1: Nitrogen = 4/10 = 40%. Mix 2: Nitrogen = 5/15 = 33.33%.
*   Combined in 5:3: Nitrogen = `(5 × 40 + 3 × 33.33)/8 = (200 + 100)/8 = 300/8 = 37.5%`.
*   Need 30%. Currently 37.5% in 8 parts of mixture. Nitrogen amount = `37.5% × 8 = 3 parts`.
*   Need: `3/Total = 30%` → Total = 10. Already have 8 parts. Add `10 - 8 = 2 parts` oxygen.
*   Ratio original : oxygen = `8 : 2 = 4 : 1`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 4:1**.

### Question 15
**Question:** 4 oranges at Rs. 10, 4 at Rs. 25, 2 at Rs. 30. Average price?
*   **Options:** `[A) 6.5  B) 15  C) 20  D) 10]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total = `4×10 + 4×25 + 2×30 = 40 + 100 + 60 = 200`. Count = 10.
*   Avg = `200/10 = 20`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 20**.
