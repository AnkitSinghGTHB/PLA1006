# Division of Wages - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./02_Time_Speed_Work_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Division of Wages (TIME AND WORK).pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Fractional Share Trick:** You don't need to find C's efficiency. If someone works for `N` days and their capacity is `D` days, they completed `N/D` of the total work, so they get exactly `N/D` of the money!

---

### Question 1
**Question:** A takes 12 days. B takes 18 days (6 more than A). A, B, C together take 5 days. Paid Rs. 9000. C's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2750  B) 3000  C) 4000  D) None of these]`
*   **Mental Trick (Fraction of Work):**
    *   Everyone worked for exactly 5 days.
    *   A completed `5/12` of the work.
    *   B completed `5/18` of the work.
    *   Let's add them up: LCM of 12 and 18 is 36. 
    *   `(15/36) + (10/36) = 25/36` of the work is done by A and B.
    *   This means C completed the remaining `11/36` of the work!
    *   C's share is therefore `11/36` of Rs. 9000.
    *   `9000 / 36 = 250`.
    *   `250 * 11 = 2750`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 2750**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Kausalya can do it in 20 days, Kaikeyi in 25 days. Joined by Sumitra and completed in 10 days. Total pay Rs. 700. Sumitra's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 130  B) 185  C) 70  D) Cannot be determined]`
*   **Mental Trick (Fraction of Work):**
    *   All three worked for exactly 10 days.
    *   Kausalya's work fraction = `10/20 = 1/2`.
    *   Kaikeyi's work fraction = `10/25 = 2/5`.
    *   Combined = `1/2 + 2/5 = 5/10 + 4/10 = 9/10`.
    *   Since Kausalya and Kaikeyi did 90% of the work, Sumitra did the last **10%** (or 1/10).
    *   Sumitra's Share = `10% of Rs. 700 = Rs. 70`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 70**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A (20 days) and B (40 days) undertake work for Rs. 450. With C, they finish in 8 days. C's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs.180  B) Rs.200  C) Rs.225  D) Rs.300]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Everyone worked for 8 days.
    *   A's work = `8/20 = 2/5`.
    *   B's work = `8/40 = 1/5`.
    *   Remaining work done by C = `1 - (2/5 + 1/5) = 1 - 3/5 = 2/5`.
    *   C gets 2/5 of the money.
    *   `(450 / 5) * 2 = 90 * 2 = 180`.
*   **Answer:** **A) Rs. 180**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Ram (6 days) and Shyam (8 days). With Rita, they finish in 3 days. Total is 800. Rita's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs.80  B) Rs.150  C) Rs.100  D) Rs.125]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Everyone worked for 3 days.
    *   Ram's work = `3/6 = 1/2`.
    *   Shyam's work = `3/8`.
    *   Combined = `1/2 + 3/8 = 4/8 + 3/8 = 7/8`.
    *   Rita's work = `1 - 7/8 = 1/8`.
    *   Rita gets `1/8` of 800.
    *   `800 / 8 = 100`.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 100**.

### Question 10
**Question:** P and Q earn Rs. 188. Q and R earn Rs. 152. P, Q, and R earn Rs. 300. Q earns daily?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs.43  B) Rs.56  C) Rs.45  D) Rs.40]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Overlap Equation):**
    *   Simply add the two pairs together: `(P + Q) + (Q + R) = 188 + 152 = 340`.
    *   Notice that this sum represents `P + 2Q + R`.
    *   We know the actual total `P + Q + R` is 300.
    *   Subtract the two: `(P + 2Q + R) - (P + Q + R) = Q`.
    *   `340 - 300 = 40`.
*   **Answer:** **D) Rs. 40**.

### Question 14
**Question:** Ramesh and Suresh complete 7/11 of task. Contract is Rs. 1100. Rajesh's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs.350  B) Rs.200  C) Rs.400  D) Rs.650]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Rajesh literally just completed whatever was left!
    *   Remaining work = `1 - 7/11 = 4/11`.
    *   Rajesh's share = `4/11` of Rs. 1100.
    *   `1100 / 11 = 100`. And `100 * 4 = 400`.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 400**.
