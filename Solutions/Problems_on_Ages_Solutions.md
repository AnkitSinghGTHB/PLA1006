### Question 1
**Question:** Dinesh is 9 yrs younger than Roshan. Ratio = 4:5. Dinesh's age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 36  B) 23  C) 29  D) Cannot be determined]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Difference = 1 part = 9. Dinesh = 4 × 9 = 36.
*   **Answer:** **A) 36 years**.

---

### Question 2
**Question:** Pranav:Qureshi = 4:5. Five years ago = 7:9. Present ages?
*   **Options:** `[A) 40,50  B) 16,20  C) 48,60  D) 20,25]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let ages = 4x, 5x. Five years ago: (4x-5)/(5x-5) = 7/9. 36x - 45 = 35x - 35. x = 10. Ages = 40, 50.
*   **Answer:** **A) 40, 50**.

### Question 3
**Question:** A is 20 yrs older than B. In 5 years A = 2B. B's present age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 15  B) 6  C) 7  D) 8]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A = B + 20. In 5 yrs: B + 25 = 2(B + 5). B + 25 = 2B + 10. B = 15.
*   **Answer:** **A) 15 years**.

---

### Question 4
**Question:** Saransh = 50, Nazma = 40. How long ago was ratio 3:2?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 30  C) 40  D) 25]`
*   **Mental Trick:** t years ago: (50-t)/(40-t) = 3/2. 100 - 2t = 120 - 3t. t = 20.
*   **Answer:** **A) 20 years**.

---

### Question 5
**Question:** 1 year ago Mano was 4× daughter's age. Now his age = (daughter's age)². Mano's age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 17  C) 11  D) 15]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let daughter now = d. Mano now = d². One year ago: d² - 1 = 4(d - 1). d² - 4d + 3 = 0. (d-1)(d-3) = 0. d = 3 (valid). Mano = 9.
*   **Answer:** **A) 9 years**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Vijay:Ajith = 1:3. In 10 years = 2:5. Vijay's age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 30  C) 40  D) 50]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Present = x, 3x. (x+10)/(3x+10) = 2/5. 5x + 50 = 6x + 20. x = 30. But wait — let me verify: 30:90, after 10 → 40:100 = 2:5 ✅.
*   **Answer:** **B) 30**. (Hmm, but let me check A) 20: 20:60, 30:70 = 3:7 ✗. Yes, 30 is correct.)
*   **Answer:** **A) 20**. Let me redo: (x+10)/(3x+10)=2/5 → 5x+50=6x+20 → x=30. Vijay=30. But that gives 30:90 now, 40:100=2:5 later ✅. The answer is 30. But option says A)20. Let me recheck: if x=20, ratio now=20:60=1:3 ✅. After 10: 30:70=3:7 ≠ 2:5. x=30 is correct.
*   **Answer:** **B) 30**.

### Question 7
**Question:** A + B = 30. Six years ago A = 5B. B's present age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 21  C) 18  D) 27]`
*   **Mental Trick:** (A - 6) = 5(B - 6). A = 5B - 24. Also A + B = 30 → 5B - 24 + B = 30 → 6B = 54 → B = 9.
*   **Answer:** **A) 9 years**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Man is 24 yrs older than son. In 2 years, his age = 2 × son's age. Son's present age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 18  B) 14  C) 22  D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** M = S + 24. In 2 yrs: S + 26 = 2(S + 2). S + 26 = 2S + 4. S = 22.
*   **Answer:** **C) 22 years**.

### Question 9
**Question:** A = B + 2. B = 2C. A + B + C = 27. B = ?
*   **Options:** `[A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10]`
*   **Mental Trick:** B = 2C, A = 2C + 2. Sum = 2C + 2 + 2C + C = 5C + 2 = 27 → C = 5. B = 10.
*   **Answer:** **D) 10**.

### Question 10
**Question:** Ages ratio 7:9:10:13. Eldest is 12 yrs older than youngest. Sandeep's age 5 years from now?
*   **Options:** `[A) 30  B) 35  C) 25  D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Diff = 13 - 7 = 6 parts = 12 years → 1 part = 2. Sandeep = 10 × 2 = 20. In 5 years = 25.
*   **Answer:** **C) 25**.

---

### Question 11
**Question:** 10 yrs ago mother = 4 × daughter. 10 yrs hence mother = 2 × daughter. Priya's present age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 25  B) 20  C) 15  D) 18]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let Priya now = P, Mother = M. M - 10 = 4(P - 10) and M + 10 = 2(P + 10).
*   From 2nd: M = 2P + 10. Sub in 1st: 2P + 10 - 10 = 4P - 40 → 2P = 40 → P = 20.
*   **Answer:** **B) 20**.

---

### Question 12
**Question:** Father = 4 × daughter. In 5 yrs = 3 × daughter. Further 5 yrs, how many times?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1.5  B) 2  C) 2.5  D) 3]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let D = x, F = 4x. In 5 yrs: 4x + 5 = 3(x + 5) → x = 10. F = 40.
*   In 10 yrs: F = 50, D = 20. Ratio = 50/20 = 2.5.
*   **Answer:** **C) 2.5 times**.

---

### Question 13
**Question:** After 20 yrs, age = 10 × age 10 yrs back. Present age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 6.2  B) 7.7  C) 13.3  D) 10]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A + 20 = 10(A - 10) → A + 20 = 10A - 100 → 120 = 9A → A = 13.33.
*   **Answer:** **C) 13.3 years**.

---

### Question 14
**Question:** 1 yr ago Honey:Piyush = 2:3. After 5 yrs = 4:5. Piyush's present age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5  B) 25  C) 10  D) 15]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 1 yr ago: 2x, 3x. Now: 2x+1, 3x+1. After 5 more: 2x+6, 3x+6. Ratio = 4:5.
*   5(2x+6) = 4(3x+6) → 10x + 30 = 12x + 24 → x = 3. Piyush now = 3(3) + 1 = 10.
*   **Answer:** **C) 10 years**.

---

### Question 15
**Question:** "I was 1/3 of your present age when you were born." Man = 84. Son's age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 45.7  B) 28  C) 59.3  D) 63]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let son's present age = S. Son was born S years ago. Father was 84 - S then.
*   Father's age then = 1/3 × son's present age: 84 - S = S/3 → 252 - 3S = S → 4S = 252 → S = 63.
*   **Answer:** **D) 63 years**.
