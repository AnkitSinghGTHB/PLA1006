### Question 1
**Question:** Two students appeared. One secured 9 marks more than the other and his score was 56% of the sum.
*   **Options:** `[a) 39,30  b) 43,32  c) 42,33  d) 43,34]`
*   **Mental Trick (Percentage Gap):** If the higher scorer got 56% of the total sum, the lower scorer must have gotten `100% - 56% = 44%` of the total sum.
*   The difference between them is exactly `56% - 44% = 12%` of the total sum.
*   We know their difference is 9 marks. So, `12% of Total = 9 marks`.
*   Mental math: Divide by 3 -> `4% = 3 marks`. Multiply by 25 -> `100% = 75 marks` (Total Sum). 
*   Now check options where the sum is 75. Only option (c) `42 + 33 = 75`.
*   **Answer:** **c) 42, 33**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Vendor sells 60%, throws 15% of remaining. Next day sells 50% of remaining and throws the rest. % thrown away?
*   **Options:** `[a) 23  b) 36  c) 17  d) 6]`
*   **Mental Trick (Base 100):** Let total apples = 100.
*   Sells 60. Remaining = 40.
*   Throws 15% of 40: `10% of 40 = 4`. Half of that is `2`. So `4+2 = 6 apples thrown`.
*   Remaining = `40 - 6 = 34`. 
*   Next day throws exactly half (50%) of the rest: `34 / 2 = 17 apples thrown`.
*   Total thrown = `6 + 17 = 23`.
*   **Answer:** **a) 23**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Peter got 30%, failed by 10 marks. Paul got 40%, got 15 marks above passing. Pass mark?
*   **Options:** `[a) 75  b) 250  c) 90  d) 85]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Scale Method):** 
*   The percentage gap between Peter (30%) and Paul (40%) is `10%`.
*   The mark gap between Peter (-10 from pass) and Paul (+15 from pass) is `25 marks`.
*   Therefore, `10% = 25 marks`. 
*   Maximum marks (100%) = 250.
*   Peter's marks = 30% of 250 = 75. Since he failed by 10, passing is `75 + 10 = 85`.
*   **Answer:** **d) 85**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Sheep grow by x% in 2000, then decrease by y% in 2001, returning to EXACTLY the original 1 million count. Which is correct?
*   **Options:** `[1. y>x  2. x=y  3. x>y  4. xy=0]`
*   **Mental Trick (Base Illusion):** To return to the same number after a percentage increase, the percentage decrease MUST be smaller. 
*   Why? Because the decrease (y%) is being applied to a *larger* base number. E.g., 100 increases by 25% to 125. To get back to 100, you only subtract 25, which is 20% of 125. 
*   Therefore, increase (x) is always greater than decrease (y).
*   **Answer:** **3. x > y**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Class B has 50% more students than A. Girls in A = Boys in B. Number of girls is same in both classes. % of boys in the group?
*   **Options:** `[a) 33.33%  b) 40%  c) 25%  d) 60%]`
*   **Mental Trick (Plug 100):** Let Total A = 100. Therefore, Total B = 150 (50% more).
*   Girls A = Girls B. Let's call them G. 
*   We know Boys in B = `150 - G`.
*   The question states Girls in A = Boys in B. So `G = 150 - G`. 
*   `2G = 150 -> G = 75`.
*   Total students = `100 + 150 = 250`. Total Girls = `75 + 75 = 150`.
*   Total Boys = `250 - 150 = 100`. 
*   Boy % = `100 / 250 = 40%`.
*   **Answer:** **b) 40%**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Party D secured 12% of TOTAL votes more than Party R. R got 132,000 votes. By how many votes did R lose?
*   **Options:** `[a) 300k  b) 168k  c) 36k  d) 24k]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let R get `r%` and D get `d%`. 
*   `d - r = 12%` and `d + r = 100%`. Mentally solve: split the remaining 88% equally (44 each) and give the extra 12 to D (44+12=56). So R=44%, D=56%.
*   R's 44% = 132,000 votes. 
*   `1% = 132 / 44` -> `1% = 3,000 votes`.
*   The loss margin is exactly 12%. So `12 * 3,000 = 36,000`.
*   **Answer:** **c) 36,000**.

### Question 13
**Question:** 2/5th voters promise A, rest promise B. On last day 15% backtrack on A, 25% backtrack on B. A lost by 200 votes. Total voters?
*   **Options:** `[a) 10000  b) 11000  c) 9000  d) 9500]`
*   **Mental Trick (Base 1000):** Let total voters = 1000.
*   Promise A = 400 (2/5th). Promise B = 600 (3/5th).
*   15% backtrack from A = `15% of 400 = 60` switch to B.
*   25% backtrack from B = `25% of 600 = 150` switch to A.
*   Final A votes = `400 - 60 + 150 = 490`. Final B votes = `600 - 150 + 60 = 510`.
*   Difference = 20 votes. 
*   We assumed 1000 voters and got a margin of 20. But the real margin is 200 (which is exactly `10x` bigger).
*   Therefore, total voters = `1000 * 10 = 10,000`.
*   **Answer:** **a) 10,000**.
