### Question 1
**Question:** Necklace & shop sold for 1 lakh each. Necklace: 20% loss. Shop: 20% profit. Net profit or loss %?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5.26%  B) 4%  C) 6.20%  D) 7.12%]`
*   **Mental Trick (Same SP, Different %):** When selling two items at the same price, one at x% profit and x% loss, there's ALWAYS a net loss = x²/100 %.
*   Loss% = 20²/100 = 400/100 = **4%**. But wait — 4% is the "common shortcut" for total SP-based. Let's verify:
*   CP of necklace = 100,000/0.8 = 125,000. CP of shop = 100,000/1.2 = 83,333.
*   Total CP = 208,333. Total SP = 200,000. Loss = 8,333. Loss% = 8,333/208,333 = 4%.
*   **Answer:** **B) 4%**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Buy 10 chocolates for Re. 1. Sell how many for Re. 1 to gain 25%?
*   **Options:** `[A) 16  B) 8  C) 4  D) 2]`
*   **Mental Trick:** CP of 1 chocolate = 1/10 = 0.10. SP with 25% profit = 0.10 × 1.25 = 0.125.
*   Chocolates per rupee = 1/0.125 = 8.
*   **Answer:** **B) 8**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Dishonest vendor buys with 1350g weight (instead of 1kg), sells with 900g weight. Claims cost price. Profit%?
*   **Options:** `[A) 30%  B) 60%  C) 50%  D) 40%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** He pays for 1000g but gets 1350g. He sells what he calls 1000g but gives only 900g.
*   Effective: pays for 1000, gets 1350. Sells 900 as 1000. From 1350g purchased, he can make 1350/900 = 1.5 "units" to sell at cost of 1 unit.
*   Profit = 0.5/1 = 50%.
*   **Answer:** **C) 50%**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Markup 50%, discount 10%, false weights reduce quantity by 20%. Profit%?
*   **Options:** `[A) 57.5%  B) 68.75%  C) 52.5%  D) 41%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let CP = 100. Marked = 150. After 10% discount SP = 135.
*   But only gives 80% of goods. Effective CP of goods given = 80. Profit = 135 - 80 = 55. Profit% = 55/80 = 68.75%.
*   **Answer:** **B) 68.75%**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Discount 70%, still gains 15%. CP = Rs. 6000. Find Marked Price.
*   **Options:** `[A) 23000  B) 24000  C) 20000  D) 18000]`
*   **Mental Trick:** SP = 1.15 × 6000 = 6900. SP = 30% of MP (since 70% discount). MP = 6900/0.3 = 23,000.
*   **Answer:** **A) 23,000**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Sells at cost price but gives 600g instead of 400g. Profit or loss %?
*   **Options:** `[A) 60% loss  B) 50% profit  C) 33.33% loss  D) 66.66% profit]`
*   **Mental Trick:** He charges for 400g but gives 600g. So he gives away 200g extra for free.
*   Loss on each transaction = 200/600 of the value given = 33.33%. (Loss is on what he gave, not what he charged.)
*   Wait — he charges for 400g at cost price. CP of 600g is higher than what he charges for 400g. Loss = (600-400)/600 = 33.33%.
*   **Answer:** **C) 33.33% loss**.

### Question 7
**Question:** Max discount on marked price to break even, if markup = 50%?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50%  B) 20%  C) 25%  D) 33.33%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Break even: SP = CP. MP = 1.5 × CP. Discount% = (MP - CP)/MP = 0.5CP/1.5CP = 1/3 = 33.33%.
*   **Answer:** **D) 33.33%**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Sells 2 apartments at equal price. One at 40% loss, other at 40% gain. Net P/L %?
*   **Options:** `[A) 15% loss  B) 16% loss  C) 16% profit  D) Neither]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Same SP, same percentage → always a loss = x²/100 = 1600/100 = 16% loss.
*   **Answer:** **B) 16% loss**.

### Question 9
**Question:** CP of 40 books = SP of 35 books. Profit or loss %?
*   **Options:** `[A) 12.5% loss  B) 14.5% profit  C) 14.2% profit  D) 12% loss]`
*   **Mental Trick:** CP of 1 book = x. SP of 1 book = 40x/35 = 8x/7. Profit = x/7. Profit% = (1/7)/1 × 100 = 14.28% ≈ 14.28%.
*   Closest: **C) 14.2% profit**. (Rounding of 100/7).
*   **Answer:** **C) 14.2% profit**.

### Question 10
**Question:** Car bought for Rs. 1250, sold at 10% profit. SP?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1375  B) 1480  C) 1275  D) 1475]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 10% of 1250 = 125. SP = 1250 + 125 = 1375.
*   **Answer:** **A) 1375**.

### Question 11
**Question:** 10 apples for $1. Sell a dozen at 25% profit. Price per dozen?
*   **Options:** `[A) $0.12  B) $1.25  C) $1.5  D) $3]`
*   **Mental Trick:** CP per apple = $0.10. SP per apple = 0.10 × 1.25 = $0.125. Per dozen = 12 × 0.125 = $1.50.
*   **Answer:** **C) $1.5**.

### Question 12
**Question:** 10% loss at Rs. 20. Price for 10% profit?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs. 23  B) Rs. 22.22  C) Rs. 24.44  D) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 90% of CP = 20, so CP = 200/9. SP for 10% profit = 110% of CP = 1.1 × 200/9 = 220/9 = Rs. 24.44.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 24.44**.

### Question 13
**Question:** 50% discount, still 10% gain. CP = Rs. 10,000. Find MP.
*   **Options:** `[A) 22000  B) 21000  C) 20000  D) 18000]`
*   **Mental Trick:** SP = 1.10 × 10,000 = 11,000. SP = 50% of MP → MP = 22,000.
*   **Answer:** **A) Rs. 22,000**.

### Question 14
**Question:** Sold at 1% gain. If sold at 1% loss, would lose Rs. 1 vs the gain scenario. Which is true?
*   **Options:** `[A) CP = 50  B) SP = 50  C) Profit = 50  D) Depends on x]`
*   **Mental Trick:** SP at 1% gain = 1.01 × CP. SP at 1% loss = 0.99 × CP.
*   Difference = 1.01CP - 0.99CP = 0.02CP = Rs. 1. CP = 50. SP at gain = 50.50.
*   **Answer:** **A) The cost price is Rs. 50**.

### Question 15
**Question:** P, Q, R invest 1:2:3. After 2 months P increases by 40%. Total profit Rs. 1900. P's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 350  B) 380  C) 400  D) 450]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let investments be 1x, 2x, 3x. P's weighted investment = 1x × 2 + 1.4x × 10 = 2x + 14x = 16x.
*   Q = 2x × 12 = 24x. R = 3x × 12 = 36x. Total = 76x.
*   P's share = (16/76) × 1900 = (4/19) × 1900 = 400.
*   **Answer:** **C) 400**.
