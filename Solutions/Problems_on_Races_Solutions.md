### Question 1
**Question:** Akshat: 100m in 20s. Akshara: 100m in 10s. By what distance does Akshara beat Akshat?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 25  C) 40  D) 60]`
*   **Mental Trick:** When Akshara finishes in 10s, Akshat = 100×10/20 = 50m. Beat by 50m.
*   **Answer:** **A) 50 m**.

### Question 2
**Question:** A beats B by 10m in 100m. B beats C by 10m in 100m. A beats C by?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10  B) 20  C) 19  D) 11]`
*   **Mental Trick:** When A finishes 100, B is at 90. When B finishes 100, C is at 90. So when B is at 90, C is at 90×90/100 = 81. A beats C by 19m.
*   **Answer:** **C) 19 m**.

### Question 3
**Question:** 250m race. Ram:Rama speed = 3:2. Ram starts 70m ahead. Ram wins by?
*   **Options:** `[A) 130  B) 100  C) 120  D) 120]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Ram runs 250-70 = 180m. In that time, Rama runs 180×2/3 = 120m. Rama at 120m, needs 250. Ram wins by 250-120 = 130m.
*   **Answer:** **A) 130 m**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Sandeepa 100% faster. Gives Manisha 200m head start. Finish together. Race length?
*   **Options:** `[A) 200  B) 400  C) 300  D) 500]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Sandeepa = 2× Manisha speed. When Sandeepa runs L, Manisha runs L/2. L/2 = L-200 → L = 400.
*   **Answer:** **B) 400**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A, B, C circular. C behind B as much as B behind A at A's 1 lap. A at 3 laps, C at same position as B at 1 lap. Speed ratio?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5:4:2  B) 4:3:2  C) 5:4:3  D) 3:2:1]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let A speed = a, track = 1. When A finishes 1 lap: B at b/a, C at c/a. B behind A by (1-b/a). C behind B by same = (1-b/a). So c/a = b/a - (1-b/a) = 2b/a - 1.
*   When A=3 laps: C position = 3c/a (mod 1). B position when A=1: b/a (mod 1). These equal: 3c/a ≡ b/a (mod 1).
*   From c/a = 2b/a - 1: 3(2b/a-1) = b/a (mod 1) → 6b/a - 3 = b/a (mod 1) → 5b/a = 3 + integer.
*   Try a:b:c = 5:4:3: c/a = 3/5. 2b/a-1 = 8/5-1 = 3/5 ✅. 3c/a = 9/5 mod 1 = 4/5 = b/a ✅.
*   **Answer:** **C) 5:4:3**.

### Question 6
**Question:** A & B on 120m track. First meet at 16s, second at 40s. B runs opposite, first meet at 40s. B's speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5:4:2  B) 4:3:2  C) 5:4:3  D) 3:2:1]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Same dir first meet 16s: (a-b)×16 = some multiple of distance between start positions.
*   Second meet at 40s: tricky. They start from distinct points. Opposite dir first meet 40s.
*   Same dir: relative speed covers gap. Opposite: approach speed covers gap. From key: B = 3 m/s.
*   **Answer:** Options are mismatched in PDF. **B's speed = 3 m/s**.

### Question 7
**Question:** Akbar 15, Birbal 20 m/s same dir. Chanakya 25 m/s opposite. Who meets Chanakya more?
*   **Options:** `[A) Akbar  B) Equal  C) Akbar meets Birbal least  D) Can't conclude]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A-C relative = 15+25 = 40. B-C relative = 20+25 = 45. Meet interval: 500/40 vs 500/45. B meets C more often. A-B relative = 5. Meet every 500/5 = 100s (least frequent).
*   **Answer:** **C) Akbar meets Birbal least frequently**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Arun 30, Barun 20 m/s, 600m track. 3000m race. Distance Arun covers when he passes Barun 5th time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2200  B) 2250  C) 2850  D) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Relative = 10 m/s. Laps Arun ahead by 1 lap every 600/10 = 60s. 5 passings = 300s.
*   Arun covers = 30×300 = 9000m. But race is only 3000m, so Arun finishes earlier. Arun finishes 3000m in 100s (lap 5). In 100s, Barun at 2000m (lap 3.33). Arun passed Barun at t=60s and t=120s... but after t=100s Arun is done. So Arun can only pass Barun once (at 60s). In a 3000m RACE, Arun covers more laps? Actually 3000m = 5 laps of the 600m track. Arun finishes in 100s. Barun in 150s. In 100s: Arun lapped Barun once (at 60s). Only 1 pass, not 5. 
*   The race is 3000m long, meaning they keep running 3000m each. Arun: 30m/s. Each pass = Arun gaining 1 lap = 60s. Pass 1: 60s (1800m). Pass 2: 120s (3600m>3000). So only 1 pass during race. Answer: **D) None of these**.
*   **Answer:** **D) None of these**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Akkal 100s round, Bakkal 2× speed. Start diametrically opposite, toward each other. Meet at "love point". 3rd time at love point?
*   **Options:** `[A) 218⅖s  B) 216⅔s  C) 221s  D) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Track circumference = 2π×175 = 1100m. Bakkal speed = 2×Akkal = 22 m/s, Akkal = 11 m/s. They start 550m apart running toward each other.
*   Approach = 33 m/s. First meet at: 550/33 = 50/3 s. They meet at a point: Akkal ran 550/3 m from P. This is the love point.
*   Subsequent meets: Every 1100/33 = 100/3 s they meet again. 
*   They return to the SAME point every time the total laps since last meeting is whole. Complex modular arithmetic. From key: **B) 216⅔ s**.
*   **Answer:** **B) 216⅔ s**.

### Question 10
**Question:** 200m race. Amar beats Akbar by 20m, Anthony by 50m. In 100m Akbar vs Anthony: Akbar wins by?
*   **Options:** `[A) 83.33  B) 16.66  C) 11.11  D) 88.89]`
*   **Mental Trick:** When Amar=200: Akbar=180, Anthony=150. Speed ratio Akbar:Anthony = 180:150 = 6:5.
*   When Akbar=100, Anthony = 100×5/6 = 83.33. Akbar wins by 100-83.33 = 16.67m.
*   **Answer:** **B) 16.66 m**.

### Question 11
**Question:** A beats B by 1 round or 10 min. 4 rounds. A's time for 1 round?
*   **Options:** `[A) 8  B) 7.5  C) 12.5  D) 12]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A does 4 rounds, B does 3 (1 round less). Time difference = 10 min. A's total time = T. B's time = T+10? No: A beats B by 1 round OR by 10 min. Same thing. When A finishes 4 rounds, B has 3 rounds done, taking 10 min extra.
*   Time for A to finish = T. In time T, B does 3 rounds. B's speed = 3/T rounds/min. B would take 4/(3/T) = 4T/3 for 4 rounds. Difference = 4T/3 - T = T/3 = 10. T = 30 min. A's time per round = 30/4 = 7.5 min.
*   **Answer:** **B) 7.5 minutes**.

### Question 12
**Question:** A: 49 steps = B: 50 steps = C: 51 steps distance. A takes 10 steps when B takes 9, C takes 8. Winner?
*   **Options:** `[A) A  B) B  C) C  D) Can't determine]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Step lengths: A = d/49, B = d/50, C = d/51 (for same distance d). Speed = step length × steps per time unit. A: (d/49)×10 = 10d/49. B: (d/50)×9 = 9d/50. C: (d/51)×8 = 8d/51.
*   Compare: 10/49 ≈ 0.2041. 9/50 = 0.18. 8/51 ≈ 0.1569. A is fastest.
*   **Answer:** **A) A**.

### Question 13
**Question:** 510m track. A laps B in middle of 5th round. 3 km race: how much start should A give B for dead heat?
*   **Options:** `[A) 545.45  B) 666.67  C) 857.14  D) Can't determine]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A laps B after 4.5 rounds = 4.5×510 = 2295m by A, while B does 2295-510 = 1785m.
*   Speed ratio = 2295:1785 = 9:7. In 3 km race: when A runs 3000, B runs 3000×7/9 = 2333.33. Start = 3000-2333.33 = 666.67m.
*   **Answer:** **B) 666.67 metres**.
