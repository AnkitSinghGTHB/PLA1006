This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Averages 2 - Weight avg.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Inclusion/Replacement Equation:** 
> *   When someone is **added**: `New Person = Old Avg + (New Total People * Increase)`
> *   When someone is **replaced**: `New Person = Old Person + (Total People * Increase)`

---

### Question 1
**Question:** Average weight of 25 students is 48kg. Teacher included, average increases by 2kg. Teacher's weight?
*   **Options:** `[A) 100 kg  B) 102 kg  C) 110 kg  D) 115 kg]`
*   **Mental Trick (Inclusion Rule):**
    *   Teacher brings their own 48kg to match the old average, PLUS enough extra weight to give everyone (including themselves) 2kg more.
    *   New total people = 26.
    *   Extra weight needed = `26 * 2kg = 52kg`.
    *   Teacher = `48 + 52 = 100kg`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 100 kg**.

### Question 3
**Question:** A man whose bowling average is 14.6 takes 5 wickets for 25 runs. Average reduces by 0.6. Wickets taken before last match?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 75  C) 100  D) 110]`
*   **Mental Trick (Alligation Cross):**
    *   Old Average = `14.6`.
    *   Last Match Average = `25 / 5 = 5.0`.
    *   New Combined Average = `14.6 - 0.6 = 14.0`.
    *   Use the cross-difference method:
        *   Left leg: `14.0 - 5.0 = 9.0` (weight for old matches)
        *   Right leg: `14.6 - 14.0 = 0.6` (weight for last match)
    *   Ratio of Wickets = `9.0 : 0.6` = `90 : 6` = `15 : 1`.
    *   The "1" part corresponds to the last match's **5 wickets**.
    *   Therefore, the "15" parts correspond to `15 * 5 = 75 wickets`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 75**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Average of 27 results is 19. Average of first thirteen is 15, last thirteen is 19. The fourteenth result is?
*   **Options:** `[A) 32  B) 53  C) 68  D) 71]`
*   **Mental Trick (Net Deviation Zero):**
    *   The overall average is 19.
    *   Deviation of first 13 items: `15 - 19 = -4`. Total = `13 * (-4) = -52`.
    *   Deviation of last 13 items: `19 - 19 = 0`. Total = `0`.
    *   Total deviation so far = `-52`.
    *   Because the overall average MUST remain 19, the 14th item must single-handedly cancel out this `-52` deficit!
    *   14th item = `Base Avg + (+52)`.
    *   `19 + 52 = 71`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 71**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Average salary of 25 employees is Rs. 6000. Manager added, average increases to 6400. Manager's salary?
*   **Options:** `[A) 15,000  B) 15,500  C) 16,400  D) 17,200]`
*   **Mental Trick (Inclusion Rule):**
    *   Manager matches the base Rs. 6000.
    *   Manager adds Rs. 400 to the pockets of all 26 people (25 employees + 1 manager).
    *   Extra money = `26 * 400 = 10,400`.
    *   Total Salary = `6000 + 10400 = 16,400`.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 16,400**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Mark wrongly entered as 60 instead of 30. Average increased by 1/3 of a mark. Number of pupils?
*   **Options:** `[A) 75  B) 84  C) 90  D) 95]`
*   **Mental Trick (Total Error Distribution):**
    *   Total error = `60 - 30 = +30` marks.
    *   This extra 30 marks was distributed evenly, giving everyone `1/3` of a mark.
    *   Number of Pupils = `Total Error / Increase per person` = `30 / (1/3)`.
    *   `30 * 3 = 90`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 90**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Average height of 40 boys (out of 60) is 170cm. Avg of remaining is 164cm. Entire class average?
*   **Options:** `[A) 152  B) 168  C) 175  D) 190]`
*   **Mental Trick (Weighted Deviation Base):**
    *   Ratio of students = `40 : 20` = `2 : 1`.
    *   Averages = `170` and `164`.
    *   Assume base average is the lower one: `164`.
    *   The 2 parts of boys have an excess of `+6` cm each. Total excess = `2 * 6 = +12`.
    *   Distribute this `12` excess across all `3` parts of the class.
    *   `12 / 3 = +4` increase to the base.
    *   Overall average = `164 + 4 = 168`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 168 cm**.
