# Simple Interest - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./01_Arithmetic_Percentages_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Simple Interest.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Effective Rate Trick:** Never use the full `I = PTR/100` formula. Simply multiply the `Time` and `Rate` to get the **Effective Percentage**, and take that percentage of the Principal.

---

### Question 2
**Question:** A sum of money will double itself in 4 years at simple interest. Yearly rate?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 25  C) 30  D) 35]`
*   **Mental Trick (The 100% Rule):** 
    *   If money doubles, you gained exactly **100%** of your original amount as interest.
    *   You gained 100% over 4 years. 
    *   Rate per year = `100% / 4 = 25%`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 25**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Steve invested $10,000 at 2% simple interest for 4 years. Interest earned?
*   **Options:** `[A) $700  B) $800  C) $900  D) $1000]`
*   **Mental Trick (Effective Rate):**
    *   Effective rate = `4 years * 2% = 8%`.
    *   Calculate 8% of 10,000. 
    *   `1% of 10k = 100`. So, `8 * 100 = 800`.
*   **Answer:** **B) $ 800**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Ryan borrowed $15,000 at 10% Simple Interest. He paid $9,000 as interest. Find the time.
*   **Options:** `[A) 4 Years  B) 5 Years  C) 6 Years  D) 7 Years]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Find his interest for exactly **1 year**.
    *   `10% of 15,000 = $1,500`.
    *   If he pays $1,500 every single year, how many years to reach $9,000?
    *   `9000 / 1500 = 6`. 
*   **Answer:** **C) 6 Years**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A sum amounts to Rs. 815 in 3 years and to Rs. 854 in 4 years. The sum is?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs. 690  B) Rs. 692  C) Rs. 698  D) Rs. 700]`
*   **Mental Trick (Difference isolation):**
    *   Because it's Simple Interest, the interest added each year is identical.
    *   Interest for 1 year = Difference from Year 3 to Year 4 = `854 - 815 = Rs. 39`.
    *   The total interest accumulated over the first 3 years = `39 * 3 = Rs. 117`.
    *   To find the base Principal, just subtract the 3-year interest from the 3-year amount.
    *   `815 - 117 = 698`.
*   **Answer:** **C) Rs. 698**.

### Question 8
**Question:** A man took a loan at 8% p.a. simple interest. After 4 years, he paid Rs. 6200 as interest. Principal?
*   **Options:** `[A) Rs. 10000  B) Rs. 20000  C) Rs. 18000  D) Rs. 19375]`
*   **Mental Trick (Effective Rate reverse):**
    *   Effective rate for 4 years = `4 * 8% = 32%`.
    *   We know 32% of his loan equals 6200.
    *   `32% = 6200`. Let's simplify. Divide by 4: `8% = 1550`.
    *   To get to 100%, multiply 8% by 12.5. 
    *   `1550 * 12.5` = `15500 (which is times 10) + 3875 (which is times 2.5)` = `19375`.
*   **Answer:** **D) Rs. 19375**.

### Question 16
**Question:** Vikram invested a certain sum at 8% p.a. SI for 'n' years. He got back 5 times his original investment. Value of n?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 52.5  C) 58  D) None]`
*   **Mental Trick (The 100% Rule again):**
    *   If you get back **5 times** your money, 1 part is your original principal, and **4 parts** are pure pure interest.
    *   Therefore, the interest generated was **400%**.
    *   If the bank gives you 8% per year, how long to reach 400%?
    *   `400 / 8 = 50 years`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 50**.

### Question 17
**Question:** The simple interest on a sum is 1/5th of the principal. The number of years is 5 times the rate of interest. Find the rate of interest (in %).
*   **Options:** `[A) 4  B) 2  C) 3  D) 1]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Interest is 1/5th. That means Total Interest is **20%**.
    *   Total Interest = `Time * Rate`. We know `Time = 5 * Rate`.
    *   So, `(5 * Rate) * Rate = 20`.
    *   `5 * Rate^2 = 20`.
    *   `Rate^2 = 4`.
    *   `Rate = 2`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 2**.
