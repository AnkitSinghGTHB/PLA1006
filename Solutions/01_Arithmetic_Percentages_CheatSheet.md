## 1. Percentages, Profit & Loss

### 🧠 Mental Trick: Fraction to Percentage Memory
*   1/2 = 50%, 1/3 = 33.33%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/6 = 16.66%
*   **Example Question:** Find 16.66% of 216.
    *   **Mental Solution:** Recognize 16.66% as `1/6`. Just do `216 / 6`. First `21/6 = 3` remainder 3. `36 / 6 = 6`. Answer: **36**. No decimal calculation needed!

### 🚀 Successive Change Shortcut
If a value increases/decreases sequentially: **Net Change = `A + B + (A*B)/100`** *(Use negative sign for drops)*
*   **Example Question:** A shopkeeper raises prices by 20%, then gives a 10% discount. What is his net profit?
    *   **Mental Solution:** A = 20, B = -10. 
    *   Formula: `20 - 10 + (20 * -10)/100` -> `10 - 200/100` -> `10 - 2` = **+8%**. He makes an 8% profit. 

### 🏷️ Marked Price (MP) & Discount Ratio
Formula: `MP / CP = (100 + Profit%) / (100 - Discount%)`
*   **Example Question:** A trader gives a 20% discount but still makes a 20% profit. What is the ratio of his Marked Price to Cost Price?
    *   **Mental Solution:** `MP / CP = (100 + 20) / (100 - 20) = 120 / 80`. Simplify `12/8` to **3/2**. So MP is 50% above CP.

## 2. Simple Interest (SI) & Compound Interest (CI)

### 📈 Mental Trick for 2-Year CI
**Effective CI% = `2R + (R^2)/100`**
*   **Example Question:** Find the compound interest on $4000 at 5% per annum for 2 years.
    *   **Mental Solution:** Effective rate = `2(5) + (5^2)/100` = `10 + 0.25` = 10.25%.
    *   Calculate 10.25% of 4000. `10% of 4000 = 400`. `0.25% is (1/4 of 1%) -> 1/4 of 40 = 10`. Total = **$410**. 

### ⚖️ The "Difference" Trick (For 2 Years Only)
**Diff = `P * (R/100)^2`**
*   **Example Question:** The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 10% is $15. Find the sum.
    *   **Mental Solution:** `Sum * (10/100)^2 = 15`. -> `Sum * (1/100) = 15`. Sum = **$1500**.

## 3. Averages

### ⚖️ The "Deviation" Method (No Summing Allowed!)
1. Guess a base. 2. Average the deviations. 3. Adjust base.
*   **Example Question:** Find the average of: 82, 85, 87, 89, 92.
    *   **Mental Solution:** Let's guess the average is **85**.
    *   Deviations from 85: `-3, 0, +2, +4, +7`. 
    *   Sum of deviations: `-3 + 2 + 4 + 7 = +10`.
    *   Average of these 5 deviations: `+10 / 5 = +2`.
    *   Final average: `85 + 2 = `**87**. (You totally avoided adding the large numbers!)

### 👤 Inclusion/Exclusion (Person joining)
**New Person's Value = `Old Average + (Total New Number of People * Change in Avg)`**
*   **Example Question:** Average weight of 19 students is 40kg. If the teacher joins, the new average becomes 41kg. What is the teacher's weight?
    *   **Mental Solution:** `40kg (old avg) + [ 20 (new total people) * +1 (increase) ] = 40 + 20 = `**60kg**.

## 4. Mixtures & Alligations

### ❌ The Cross Trick
*   **Example Question:** In what ratio must grocery A (costing $20/kg) be mixed with grocery B (costing $30/kg) to produce a mixture costing $24/kg?
    *   **Mental Solution:**
```text
(A) 20                       30 (B)
         \                 /
            Mixture (24)
         /                 \
|30-24| = 6               |20-24| = 4
```
**Ratio = 6 : 4 = 3 : 2**. (Take 3 parts of A and 2 parts of B).

## 5. Ratio & Partnership

### 🤝 The Golden Rule of Partnership
**Profit Ratio = `(Investment_A * Time_A) : (Investment_B * Time_B)`**
*   **Example Question:** Person X invests ₹50,000 for 12 months. Person Y joins later, investing ₹60,000 for 8 months. What is the profit ratio?
    *   **Mental Solution:** Instantly cancel the four zeros. 
    *   X: `5 * 12 = 60`
    *   Y: `6 * 8 = 48`
    *   Ratio: 60 : 48 -> Divide by 12 -> **5 : 4**.
