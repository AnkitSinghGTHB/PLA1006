### Question 1
**Question:** A park in the form of a right-angled triangle has a base and height of 10 m and 15 m respectively. Find the area of the park?
*   **Options:** `[A) 45  B) 75  C) 60  D) 150]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 15 = 75.
*   **Answer:** **B) 75**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Let the length of the side be 4√3 cm. What is the height of an equilateral triangle? What is the area? What is the area of a regular hexagon? What is the perimeter of a regular hexagon?
*   **Mental Trick:** Height = √3/2 × side = √3/2 × 4√3 = 6. Area = √3/4 × side² = √3/4 × 48 = 12√3. Hexagon area = 3√3/2 × side² = 72√3. Perimeter = 6 × 4√3 = 24√3.
*   **Answer:** All formulas yield correct matching values (A, B, C, D respectively).

### Question 3
**Question:** Mr. Frankenstein wants to build a house with 7 rooms each having equal area. The length of each room is 60 m, breadth is 50 m and height is 20 m. For every unit of area, he requires 120 bricks. Tiles with the dimensions 3 m x 2 m are fixed only on the walls. What is the floor area? Volume? How many tiles?
*   **Mental Trick:** Floor Area = 60 × 50 = 3000 m². Volume = 60 × 50 × 20 = 60,000 m³. Wall Area = 2 × (60 + 50) × 20 = 4400 m². Tile Area = 6 m². Tiles required = 4400 / 6 ≈ 733.33 (so 734 tiles).
*   **Answer:** Floor = **3000 m²**, Volume = **60,000 m³**, Tiles ≈ **734**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Let the diameter be 14 cm and height be 6 cm. What is the volume of the cylinder? Volume of the cone? Total surface area of the cylinder?
*   **Mental Trick:** r = 7. Cylinder Volume = πr²h = (22/7) × 49 × 6 = 924 cm³. Cone Volume = 924 / 3 = 308 cm³. TSA Cylinder = 2πr(r + h) = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × (7 + 6) = 44 × 13 = 572 cm².
*   **Answer:** Cylinder vol = **924 cm³**, Cone = **308 cm³**, TSA = **572 cm²**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A triangle is made from a rope. The sides of triangle are 19 cm, 14 cm and 31 cm. What will be the area of the square made from the rope?
*   **Mental Trick:** Total length of rope (Perimeter) = 19 + 14 + 31 = 64 cm. Side of square = 64 / 4 = 16 cm. Area = 16² = 256.
*   **Answer:** **256 cm²**.

### Question 6
**Question:** How many bricks each of dimension 13 cm x 12 cm x 19 cm are required to construct a wall of dimensions 390 cm x 400 cm x 410 cm, if the wall constitutes 24% of sand and cement?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20,000  B) 14,800  C) 18,200  D) 16,400]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Volume of one brick = 13 × 12 × 19 = 2964. Total wall volume = 390 × 400 × 410 = 63,960,000. Brick volume fraction = 76%. Net brick volume = 0.76 × 63,960,000 = 48,609,600. Number of bricks = 48,609,600 / 2964 ≈ 16,400.
*   **Answer:** **D) 16,400**.

### Question 7
**Question:** Find the height of a cylinder whose radius is 7 cm and the total surface area is 968 cm².
*   **Options:** `[A) 15cm  B) 14 cm  C) 12 cm  D) 16 cm]`
*   **Mental Trick:** TSA = 2πr(r + h) = 968. 2 × (22/7) × 7 × (7 + h) = 968. 44(7 + h) = 968. 7 + h = 22. h = 15.
*   **Answer:** **A) 15 cm**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Find the height of a cuboid whose volume is 275 cm³ and base area is 25 cm².
*   **Options:** `[A) 17  B) 9  C) 11  D) 13]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Volume = Base Area × height. 275 = 25 × h. h = 275 / 25 = 11.
*   **Answer:** **C) 11**.

### Question 9
**Question:** A rectangular piece of paper 11 cm × 4 cm is folded without overlapping to make a cylinder of height 4 cm. Find the volume of the cylinder.
*   **Options:** `[A) 27.5cm³  B) 38.5cm³  C) 25.3cm³  D) 11.5cm³]`
*   **Mental Trick:** The circumference becomes the length 11 cm. 2πr = 11 → r = 11 / 2π = 7/4. Volume = πr²h = (22/7) × (49/16) × 4 = 38.5.
*   **Answer:** **B) 38.5 cm³**.

### Question 10
**Question:** The area of a trapezium shaped field is 480 m², the distance between two parallel sides is 15 m and one of the parallel sides is 20 m. Find the other parallel side.
*   **Options:** `[A) 40m  B) 42m  C) 39m  D) 44m]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Area = ½(a + b)h. 480 = ½(20 + x) × 15. 960 / 15 = 64. 20 + x = 64. x = 44.
*   **Answer:** **D) 44 m**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Suppose a quadrilateral having a diagonal of length 10 cm, which divides the quadrilateral into two triangles and the heights of triangles with diagonals as the base, are 4 cm and 6 cm. Find the area of the quadrilateral.

### Question 13
**Question:** If each edge of a cube is increased by 10 %, what would be the percentage increase in volume?
*   **Options:** `[A) 33.1%  B) 11.1%  C) 22.1%  D) 25.5%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Volume is cubed. (1.1)³ = 1.331. The increase is 0.331 or 33.1%.
*   **Answer:** **A) 33.1%**.

### Question 14
**Question:** From a cylindrical vessel having a radius of the base of 7 cm and a height 6cm, water is poured into small hemispherical bowls each of radius 3.5 cm. Find the minimum number of bowls that would be required to empty the cylindrical vessel.
*   **Options:** `[A) 13  B) 11  C) 14  D) 16]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Cylinder Vol = π(7)²(6) = 294π. Hemisphere Vol = (2/3)π(3.5)³ = (2/3)π(42.875) = 28.583π. Bowls = 294π / 28.583π ≈ 10.28. We need 11 bowls to empty the cylinder completely.
*   **Answer:** **B) 11**.

### Question 15
**Question:** A solid metallic sphere of radius 26 cm is melted and recast into a cone having diameter of the base as 26 cm. What is the height of the cone?
*   **Options:** `[A) 200 cm  B) 392 cm  C) 416 cm  D) 304 cm]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Sphere Vol = Cone Vol. 4/3π(26)³ = 1/3π(13)²h. 4 × (26³ / 13²) = h. 4 × (26/13)² × 26 = h. 4 × 4 × 26 = 416.
*   **Answer:** **C) 416 cm**.

### Question 22
**Question:** Find the length of the longest pole that can be placed in a room 12 m long, 8m broad and 9 m high.

### Question 23
**Question:** If the radius of a cylinder is doubled and the height remains same, the volume will be
*   **Options:** `[A) Doubled  B) Halved  C) same  D) Quadrupled]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Volume = πr²h. If r is doubled, (2r)² = 4r², making the volume 4 times larger (quadrupled).
*   **Answer:** **D) Quadrupled**.

### Question 24
**Question:** A polygon has 27 diagonals. The number of sides of the polygon is
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 10  C) 11  D) 12]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Diagonals = n(n-3)/2. So 27 = n(n-3)/2 → n² - 3n - 54 = 0. Solving gives n = 9.
*   **Answer:** **A) 9**.

### Question 26
**Question:** A contractor undertakes the job of fencing a rectangular field of length 100 m and breadth 50 m. Find the total cost of fencing if 10 % of the amount paid to the contractor is paid as tax to the land authority.

### Question 27
**Question:** If the length of the side of the rhombus is 10 cm and the length of the shorter diagonal is 12 cm then what is the area of the rhombus?

### Question 28
**Question:** Water flows into a tank 200 m × 150 m through a rectangular pipe 1.5 m × 1.25 m @ 20 kmph. In what time (in minutes) will the water rise by 2 meters?
*   **Options:** `[A) 234 min  B) 1.2 hours  C) 9 hours  D) 96 min]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Target volume = 200 × 150 × 2 = 60,000 m³. Flow rate per minute = 1.5 × 1.25 × (20,000/60) = 625 m³/min. Time = 60,000 / 625 = 96 mins.
*   **Answer:** **D) 96 min**.

### Question 29
**Question:** A hemispherical bowl of internal radius 9 cm contains a liquid. This liquid is to be filled into cylindrical shaped small bottles of diameter 3 cm and height 4 cm. How many bottles will be needed to empty the bowl?

### Question 30
**Question:** Two metallic right circular cones having their heights 4.1 cm and 4.3 cm and the radii of their bases 2.1 cm each, have been melted together and recast into a sphere. Find the diameter of the sphere.
*   **Options:** `[A) 4 cm  B) 4.1 cm  C) 4.2 cm  D) 4.3 cm]`

*   **Answer:** **C) 4.2 cm**.

### Question 32
**Question:** Which of the following cannot be the ratio of the angles of a right-angled triangle:
*   **Options:** `[A) 1 : 2 : 3  B) 1 : 1 : 2  C) 5 : 6 : 7  D) 1 : 5 : 6]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A right triangle must have one 90° angle, meaning the largest part of the ratio must equal the sum of the other two parts, OR one part corresponds to 90°. In C) 5:6:7, sum = 18. Largest angle = 180 × (7/18) = 70° (not 90). So it cannot be right-angled.
*   **Answer:** **C) 5 : 6 : 7**.

### Question 50
**Question:** If the volume and curved surface area of a cylinder are 616m³ and 352m² respectively. what is the total surface area of the cylinder (in m²)
*   **Options:** `[A) 429  B) 419  C) 435  D) 421]`
*   **Mental Trick:** V/CSA = (πr²h)/(2πrh) = r/2. So 616/352 = r/2 → r = 2 × 616 / 352 = 3.5. CSA = 352 = 2πrh → h = 352 / (2 × 22/7 × 3.5) = 16. TSA = 2πr(r+h) = 2 × (22/7) × 3.5 × 19.5 = 22 × 19.5 = 429.
*   **Answer:** **A) 429**.
