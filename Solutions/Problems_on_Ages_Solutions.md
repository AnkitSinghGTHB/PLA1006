# Problems on Ages - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./01_Arithmetic_Percentages_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Problems on Ages.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Gap Balancing Trick:** The age difference between two people *never* changes. If the ratio differences don't match, cross-multiply their gaps to make them equal! 

---

### Question 1
**Question:** Dinesh is younger to Roshan by 9 years. If their ages are in the ratio of 4:5, how old is Dinesh?
*   **Options:** `[A) 36 years  B) 23 years  C) 29 years  D) Cannot be determined]`
*   **Mental Trick (Ratio Difference):**
    *   Ratio is 4:5. The numerical difference in parts is `5 - 4 = 1 part`.
    *   The problem says the difference in actual years is 9.
    *   So, `1 part = 9 years`.
    *   Dinesh is the younger one, so he gets 4 parts. `4 * 9 = 36`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 36 years**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Present ratio of Pranav and Qureshi is 4:5. Five years ago, the ratio was 7:9. Find their present ages.
*   **Options:** `[A) 40, 50  B) 16, 20  C) 48, 60  D) 20, 25]`
*   **Mental Trick (Gap Balancing):**
    *   Past Gap = `9 - 7 = 2`.
    *   Present Gap = `5 - 4 = 1`.
    *   Since age gaps must be identical in reality, we equalize the parts! Multiply the Present ratio by 2.
    *   New Present Ratio = **8 : 10**. Past Ratio = **7 : 9**.
    *   Now check vertical drop: From 8 parts to 7 parts is a drop of exactly **1 part**.
    *   This 1 part drop corresponds exactly to the **5 years** backwards in time!
    *   Therefore, `1 part = 5 years`.
    *   Present ages = `8 * 5` and `10 * 5`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 40, 50**.

### Question 5
**Question:** One year ago, Mano was 4 times as old as his daughter. Now his age is the square of his daughter's age. Find Mano's age.
*   **Options:** `[A) 9 years  B) 17 years  C) 11 years  D) 15 years]`
*   **Mental Trick (Option Eliminator):**
    *   Look at the condition: *"Now his age is the square of his daughter's age"*.
    *   This means Mano's current age must be a **perfect square**!
    *   Instantly scan the options: 9, 17, 11, 15. The ONLY perfect square is 9.
    *   Let's verify: If Mano is 9, Daughter is 3. One year ago they were 8 and 2. Is 8 = 4*2? Yes!
*   **Answer:** **A) 9 years**.

### Question 7
**Question:** The sum of present ages of A and B is 30. Six years ago, A was 5 times B. Present age of B?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9 years  B) 21 years  C) 18 years  D) 27 years]`
*   **Mental Trick (Time Shift):**
    *   Shift the sum to the past. Since there are 2 people, they both lose 6 years.
    *   Sum 6 years ago = `30 - 6 - 6 = 18 years`.
    *   At that time, A was 5 times B. Ratio was `5 : 1`.
    *   Total parts = `6 parts`.
    *   `6 parts = 18 years`  →  `1 part = 3 years`.
    *   So, 6 years ago, B was exactly 3 years old.
    *   Bring B back to the present: `3 + 6 = 9`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 9 years**.

### Question 9
**Question:** A is two years older than B, who is twice as old as C. Sum of A, B, C is 27. How old is B?
*   **Options:** `[A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10]`
*   **Mental Trick (Back-Testing):** Take the options from the middle.
    *   Let's test option D (10). If B = 10:
    *   B is twice C, so C = 5.
    *   A is 2 years older than B, so A = 12.
    *   Sum = `12 + 10 + 5 = 27`. Matches exactly instantly!
*   **Answer:** **D) 10**.

### Question 11
**Question:** 10 years ago, Mother was 4 times Daughter. 10 years hence, Mother will be twice Daughter. Present age of Priya (Daughter)?
*   **Options:** `[A) 25  B) 20  C) 15  D) 18]`
*   **Mental Trick (Gap Balancing):**
    *   Past ratio (-10 yrs): `4 : 1`. (Gap = 3)
    *   Future ratio (+10 yrs): `2 : 1`. (Gap = 1)
    *   Balance the gaps: Multiply future by 3!
    *   New Future ratio = **6 : 3**. (Past is still **4 : 1**).
    *   Look at Priya's parts: She goes from 1 part to 3 parts. Growth = **2 parts**.
    *   The actual time shift from -10 yrs to +10 yrs is **20 years**.
    *   `2 parts = 20 years`  →  `1 part = 10 years`.
    *   Priya's past age (1 part) = 10 years.
    *   Her present age = Past age + 10 = `10 + 10 = 20`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 20**.

### Question 15
**Question:** Man says to son, "I was one-third of your present age when you were born". Present age of the man is 84. Son's age?
*   **Options:** `[A) 45.7  B) 28  C) 59.3  D) 63]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Birth Age Rule):**
    *   A father's age when his son was born is exactly `(Father's Present Age) - (Son's Present Age)`.
    *   Let Son's age = `S`.
    *   Age at birth = `84 - S`.
    *   Problem says: Age at birth is 1/3 of Son's present age.
    *   `84 - S = 1/3 * S`
    *   `84 = S + (1/3)S = 4/3 S`
    *   `S = (84 * 3) / 4`. Cancel 84 and 4 into 21. `21 * 3 = 63`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 63 years**.
