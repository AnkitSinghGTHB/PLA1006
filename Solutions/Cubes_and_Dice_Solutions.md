### Question 1
**Question:** 8cm cube painted R/B/Blk on opposite pairs. Cut into 2cm cubes. No face painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 1  b) 4  c) 8  d) 27]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 8/2 = 4 cubes per side. Inner cubes = (4-2)³ = 2³ = 8.
*   **Answer:** **c) 8**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Only 1 face painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 18  b) 22  c) 24  d) 8]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Face center cubes: 6 faces × (4-2)² = 6×4 = 24.
*   **Answer:** **c) 24**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Only 2 faces painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 8  b) 16  c) 20  d) 24]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Edge cubes (not corners): 12 edges × (4-2) = 12×2 = 24.
*   **Answer:** **d) 24**.

### Question 4
**Question:** 64 cubes (4×4×4). Red on 2 adj, Black opposite to red, Green remaining. Only 1 face coloured?
*   **Options:** `[a) 16  b) 24  c) 28  d) 32]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Face centers: 6 × (4-2)² = 6×4 = 24.
*   **Answer:** **b) 24**.

### Question 5
**Question:** At least 1 side red?
*   **Options:** `[a) 16  b) 24  c) 28  d) 32]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Red on 2 adjacent faces. Each face has 4×4=16 cubes. Shared edge = 4 cubes (counted in both). Total = 16+16-4 = 28.
*   **Answer:** **c) 28**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Red on one side, black on OPPOSITE side?
*   **Options:** `[a) 0  b) 4  c) 8  d) 16]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Red and black are on opposite faces of the big cube (since black is opposite to the 2 red faces). A small cube can have red on one face and black on opposite only if it sits on the edge between a red face and its opposite black face. But red and black faces are opposite → no small cube touches both. **0**.
*   **Answer:** **a) 0**.

### Question 7
**Question:** 3 sides painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 24  b) 8  c) 16  d) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Corner cubes: always 8.
*   **Answer:** **b) 8**.

### Question 8
**Question:** 1 face green + adjacent face black or red?
*   **Options:** `[a) 8  b) 16  c) 24  d) 28]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Green faces are adjacent to both red and black faces. Edge cubes between green-red and green-black. Each green face has 2 edges touching red, 2 touching black = 4 edges × 2 green faces. But need: green on one face AND adjacent is black/red. Edge cubes: 4 edges (green-red) × (4-2) + 4 edges (green-black) × (4-2) = 8+8 = 16.
*   **Answer:** **b) 16**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Circle, cross, triangle, square, ellipse on 5 faces (top blank). Ellipse between cross and triangle. Square on triangle's right. Ellipse and square opposite. Where is circle?
*   **Options:** `[a) Left of triangle  b) Top  c) Bottom  d) Opposite triangle]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Ellipse opposite square. Ellipse between cross and triangle → all three are in a strip. Square is right of triangle. Top = blank. Circle must be on remaining face = bottom or opposite to cross. Circle is at bottom.
*   **Answer:** **c) Bottom face**.

### Question 10
**Question:** How many arrangements possible?
*   **Options:** `[a) 1  b) 2  c) 3  d) 4]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Given constraints fix most positions. Cross can be on either side of the strip (left or right of ellipse relative to triangle). **b) Two**.
*   **Answer:** **b) Two**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Ellipse faces north. What faces west?
*   **Options:** `[a) Square  b) Triangle  c) Circle  d) Cross]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Ellipse faces north → square faces south (opposite). Ellipse between cross and triangle (east-west). Triangle or cross faces west depending on arrangement. From the constraint "square right of triangle" and maintaining orientation: **d) Cross** or **b) Triangle**. Answer: **d) Cross**.
*   **Answer:** **d) Cross**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Rolled to get ellipse on top. Bottom?
*   **Options:** `[a) Square  b) Circle  c) Blank  d) Triangle]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Ellipse opposite square. If ellipse on top, square is bottom.
*   **Answer:** **a) Square**.

### Question 13
**Question:** Hollow 5cm cube made of 1cm cubes. 4 faces painted. Unpainted small faces?
*   **Options:** `[a) 800  b) 488  c) 900  d) 500]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Hollow cube: outer shell. Total 1cm cubes in shell = 5³-3³ = 125-27 = 98 cubes. Each cube has 6 faces = 588 total faces. 4 outer faces painted: 4×5² = 100 face-units painted. But also 2 unpainted outer faces: 2×25 = 50 face-units on outer but unpainted. Internal faces of hollow: 6 inner surfaces of 3×3 = 54 faces... This is complex. From key: **b) 488**.
*   **Answer:** **b) 488**.

### Question 14
**Question:** 9cm cube, painted G/R/Y opposite pairs, cut 3cm. None painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 1  b) 6  c) 8  d) 4]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 9/3 = 3 per side. Inner = (3-2)³ = 1.
*   **Answer:** **a) 1**.

### Question 15
**Question:** 3 faces painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 2  b) 4  c) 8  d) 12]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Corners of 3×3×3 = 8.
*   **Answer:** **c) 8**.

### Question 16
**Question:** 4 faces painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 0  b) 1  c) 2  d) 3]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Cutting a painted cube with straight cuts can give at most 3 painted faces (corner piece). 4 painted faces impossible with standard cutting.
*   **Answer:** **a) 0**.

### Question 17
**Question:** Least cuts for 84 pieces from a cube?
*   **Options:** `[a) 11  b) 12  c) 13  d) 10]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 84 = (L+1)(B+1)(H+1). Minimize L+B+H. 84 = 2×6×7 → cuts = 1+5+6 = 12. Or 3×4×7 = 2+3+6 = 11. Or 4×3×7 same. Can we do better? 84 = 2×2×21 → 1+1+20=22. No. 84 = 3×4×7 → 11.
*   **Answer:** **a) 11**.

### Question 18
**Question:** 12cm cube, red 2 opp, blue 2 opp, black 1, unpainted 1. Cut 1cm. 0-face painted?
*   **Options:** `[a) 1100  b) 1000  c) 900  d) 800]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Inner cubes = (12-2)³ = 1000. But one face is unpainted → cubes touching that face's interior also have 0 paint on that face. Inner = cubes not touching ANY painted face. 5 faces painted, 1 unpainted. Unpainted face contributes extra cubes: 10×10 face of inner cubes that touch the unpainted outer face. These weren't removed from the "inner" count since they're already inner. Actually inner = cubes not touching any of the 6 faces = 10³ = 1000. These have 0 paint regardless. **b) 1000**.
*   **Answer:** **b) 1000**.

### Question 19
**Question:** 20cm blue cube, 4cm yellow strip on all edges, all faces. Cut into 125 (5×5×5 = 4cm each). At least 2 yellow faces?
*   **Options:** `[a) 125  b) 44  c) 54  d) 27]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 20/4 = 5 per side. Yellow strip 4cm = 1 cube width. Edge cubes and corner cubes have yellow. At least 2 yellow faces: corner cubes (8, 3 yellow) + edge cubes (12×3=36, 2 yellow) = 44. Wait: edge cubes between corners: each edge has 5-2=3 edge cubes. 12×3 = 36. Corners = 8. Total ≥2 yellow = 36+8 = 44.
*   **Answer:** **b) 44**.

### Question 20
**Question:** 125 cubes (5×5×5). Remove corner columns (4 columns of 5). Color exposed faces (except bottom). 0 colored faces?
*   **Options:** `[a) 125  b) 44  c) 54  d) 36]`
*   **Mental Trick:** After removing 4 corners (20 cubes), 105 remain. Bottom face not colored. Interior cubes with no exposure: inner cubes that aren't on any surface. From key: **d) 36**.
*   **Answer:** **d) 36**.
