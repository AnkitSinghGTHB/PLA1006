### Question 1
**Question:** A and B invest 3:2. 5% goes to charity. A's share = Rs. 855. B's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 570  B) 580  C) 590  D) 600]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A:B = 3:2. A's share = 3 parts = 855 → 1 part = 285. B = 2 × 285 = 570.
*   **Answer:** **A) 570**.

---

### Question 2
**Question:** A, B, C invest 3:3:4. After 4 months A increases by 50%. Total profit Rs. 1100. B's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 300  B) 360  C) 240  D) 320]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A = 3×4 + 4.5×8 = 12 + 36 = 48. B = 3×12 = 36. C = 4×12 = 48. Total = 132.
*   B's share = 36/132 × 1100 = 3/11 × 1100 = 300.
*   **Answer:** **A) 300**.

### Question 3
**Question:** A: Rs.6500 × 6mo. B: Rs.8400 × 5mo. C: Rs.10000 × 3mo. A gets extra 5% as working partner. Profit = Rs.7400. B's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1900  B) 2660  C) 2800  D) 2840]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A's working fee = 5% of 7400 = 370. Remaining = 7030.
*   Ratios: A = 6500×6 = 39000. B = 8400×5 = 42000. C = 10000×3 = 30000. Total = 111000.
*   B's share = 42000/111000 × 7030 = 42/111 × 7030 = 14/37 × 7030 = 2660.
*   **Answer:** **B) 2660**.

### Question 4
**Question:** P, Q, R invest 1:2:3. After 2 months P increases by 40%. Profit = Rs.1900. P's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 350  B) 380  C) 400  D) 450]`
*   **Mental Trick:** P = 1×2 + 1.4×10 = 16. Q = 2×12 = 24. R = 3×12 = 36. Total = 76.
*   P's share = 16/76 × 1900 = 4/19 × 1900 = 400.
*   **Answer:** **C) 400**.

### Question 10
**Question:** A invests Rs.85,000. B joins later with Rs.42,500. Profit split 3:1. How long did B join for?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 8]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A = 85000×12. B = 42500×m. Ratio = 85000×12 : 42500×m = 3:1.
*   (85000×12)/(42500×m) = 3. 24/m = 3. m = 8 months.
*   **Answer:** **D) 8 months**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Aman: Rs.70K for 36 months. Rakhi: Rs.1.05L for 30 months. Sagar: Rs.1.4L for 24 months. Ratio?
*   **Options:** `[A) 7:6:10  B) 12:15:16  C) 42:45:56  D) Cannot determine]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Aman = 70×36 = 2520. Rakhi = 105×30 = 3150. Sagar = 140×24 = 3360.
*   Divide by 70: 36 : 45 : 48. Divide by 3: 12:15:16.
*   **Answer:** **B) 12:15:16**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Arun Rs.8000 (6mo), Kamal Rs.4000 (8mo), Vinay Rs.8000 (8mo). Gain Rs.4005. Kamal's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 890  B) 1335  C) 1602  D) 1780]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Arun = 8000×6 = 48K. Kamal = 4000×8 = 32K. Vinay = 8000×8 = 64K. Total = 144K.
*   Kamal = 32/144 × 4005 = 2/9 × 4005 = 890.
*   **Answer:** **A) Rs. 890**.

### Question 13

### Question 14
**Question:** A starts with Rs.3500. After 5 months B joins. Profit ratio = 2:3. B's capital?
*   **Options:** `[A) 7500  B) 8000  C) 8500  D) 9000]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A = 3500×12 = 42000. B = x×7. Ratio: 42000/(7x) = 2/3. 7x = 63000. x = 9000.
*   **Answer:** **D) Rs. 9000**.

### Question 15
**Question:** A & B invest 3:5. C joins after 6 months with amount = B's. Profit ratio?
*   **Options:** `[A) 3:5:2  B) 3:5:5  C) 6:10:5  D) Data inadequate]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A = 3×12 = 36. B = 5×12 = 60. C = 5×6 = 30. Ratio = 36:60:30 = 6:10:5.
*   **Answer:** **C) 6:10:5**.
