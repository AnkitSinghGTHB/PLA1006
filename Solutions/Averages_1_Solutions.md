### Question 1
**Question:** You along with 5 friends (6 total). Amounts: Rs. 31,348, Rs. 26,348, Rs. 23,348, Rs. 27,348, Rs. 32,348 and Rs. 35,348. What is the average amount?
*   **Mental Trick:** Notice the constant `348`. Ignore it for now. We have `31k, 26k, 23k, 27k, 32k, 35k`. Pick a base like **30k**.
*   **Deviations from 30k:** `+1k`, `-4k`, `-7k`, `-3k`, `+2k`, `+5k`. 
*   **Sum of Deviations:** `1-4-7-3+2+5` = `-6k`. 
*   **Average Deviation:** `-6k / 6 people` = `-1k`.
*   **Final Average:** Base `30k - 1k` = `29k`. Add back the 348.
*   **Answer:** **Rs. 29,348**. (You avoided massive addition completely).

### Question 3
**Question:** A pupil's mark was wrongly entered as 83 instead of 63. Due to that, the average mark of the class increased by half a mark. Find total pupils.
*   **Options:** `[A) 40, B) 20, C) 10, D) 73]`
*   **Mental Trick (Impact balancing):** Look at the raw error: `83 - 63 = +20 extra marks`. 
*   These 20 extra marks were distributed across the whole class, giving everyone `+0.5` marks.
*   Total students = `Total Extra Marks / Extra per student`
*   `20 / 0.5 = 40`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 40**.

### Question 4
**Question:** In first 5 overs, run rate was 4.6. Target is 183 in 20 overs. What should the remaining run rate be?
*   **Options:** `[A) 6.25, B) 10.5, C) 10.67, D) 10.75]`
*   **Mental Trick (Rounding):** Runs scored = `5 * 4.6`. (`5 * 4 = 20`, `5 * 0.6 = 3`). So 23 runs.
*   Remaining runs = `183 - 23 = 160`. Remaining overs = 15.
*   Calculation: `160 / 15` -> `32 / 3` -> 10.666...
*   **Answer:** **C) 10.67**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A student weighing 45kgs left the class, remaining 59 students' average increased by 200g (0.2kg). Old average weight? *(Assuming question asks for old average based on options)*
*   **Options:** `[A) 57, B) 69, C) 54]`
*   **Mental Trick (Transfer of Value):** When the 45kg person left, everyone else got heavier. That means the person who left was *pulling the average down*. 
*   He gave back `0.2kg` to 59 people. Total weight left behind = `59 * 0.2 = 11.8kg`.
*   Since he left with 45kg but surrendered 11.8kg of the pool to everyone else, the *new* average is exactly `45 + 11.8 = 56.8kg`. The old average (before the 0.2kg boost) was `56.8 + 0.2 = 57kg`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 57**.

### Question 6
**Question:** The average score of 70 students is 35. Average of first 28 students is 53. Average of remaining students?
*   **Options:** `[A) 42, B) 23, C) 53, D) 67]`
*   **Mental Trick (Deviation Balancing):** The base is 35. 
*   The first 28 students have an average of 53, creating an *excess* deviation of `+18` per student. Total excess = `+18 * 28`.
*   This massive excess must be perfectly balanced (absorbed) by the remaining 42 students. 
*   Their deviation must be: `-(18 * 28) / 42`. 
*   Simplify the fraction `28/42` instantly to `2/3`. 
*   Deviation = `18 * (2/3) = -12`. 
*   Subtract from base: `35 - 12 = 23`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 23**.

### Question 7
**Question:** Team of 11: Captain is 26, Wicket Keeper is 29. Excluding them, average of remaining 9 drops by 1. Find team average.
*   **Options:** `[A) 23, B) 22, C) 21, D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Sum of the two departing players is `26 + 29 = 55`.
*   If they were exactly the same as the team average, they would have carried away `2 * T`. Because they left, the remaining 9 guys had to "pay" to cover the gap. Each of the 9 guys lost 1 point (Total = 9).
*   Therefore, the two guys took away their normal share (`2T`) PLUS the 9 stolen points.
*   `2T + 9 = 55` -> `2T = 46` -> `T = 23`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 23**.

### Question 8
**Question:** 600 men, 400 women. Overall Avg wage = Rs. 25.50. Woman gets Rs. 5 less than man. Women's wage?
*   **Options:** `[A) 25, B) 22.5, C) 24, D) 21.5]`
*   **Mental Trick (Alligation Line):** Ratio of Men:Women = 600:400 = `3 : 2`.
*   The overall average (25.5) divides the gap between Men and Women in the reverse ratio `2 : 3`.
*   The total gap is Rs. 5. This 5 needs to be split into 5 parts (2+3). So 1 part = Rs 1.
*   Therefore, Men are `2 * 1 = 2` rupees above the average. Women are `3 * 1 = 3` rupees below the average.
*   Women's wage = `25.5 - 3 = 22.5`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 22.5**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Avg of H, W, C 3 years ago was 27. Avg of W, C 5 years ago was 20. Present age of H?
*   **Options:** `[A) 40, B) 15, C) 20, D) 30]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Convert everything to *current* ages instantly. 
*   H+W+C current sum = `(27 + 3) * 3 people` = `30 * 3 = 90`.
*   W+C current sum = `(20 + 5) * 2 people` = `25 * 2 = 50`.
*   H = `90 - 50` = `40`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 40**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Library has 510 visitors on Sundays, 240 on others. 30-day month starting on Sunday. Average per day?
*   **Options:** `[A) 290, B) 300, C) 285, D) 230]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A 30-day month starting on Sunday has 5 Sundays and 25 other days. 
*   Ratio of Sundays to Other Days = `5 : 25 = 1 : 5`.
*   Use Weighted Average jumping. Base average = 240. The Sunday deviation is `+270`.
*   Spread this 270 across the whole 6-part ratio (`1+5`). `270 / 6 = 45`.
*   Final average = `240 + 45 = 285`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 285**.
