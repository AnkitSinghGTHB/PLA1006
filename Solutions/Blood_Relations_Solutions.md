### Question 1
**Question:** From the family info: Leena is the _____ of Rajesh?
*   **Mental Trick:** Jai is Rajesh's son. Leena is Jai's wife. So Leena is Rajesh's daughter-in-law.
*   **Answer:** **Daughter-in-law**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Who is aunt of Jagdish?
*   **Mental Trick:** Jagdish is Leena's son. Leena married Jai. Neeta is Leena's sister-in-law (Jai's sister). Neeta is Jagdish's aunt.
*   **Answer:** **Neeta**.

### Question 3
**Question:** "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is man related to boy?
*   **Mental Trick:** Only son of my mother = me. Son of me = my son. Boy is the man's son.
*   **Answer:** **Father** (man is the father of the boy).

### Question 4
**Question:** "I met the only brother of the daughter of my grandmother." Whom did Rama meet?
*   **Mental Trick:** Grandmother's daughter = Rama's mother or aunt. Only brother of that daughter = Rama's maternal uncle or father. If grandmother's daughter is Rama's mother, her only brother = Rama's maternal uncle. But Rama is telling Lakshmana, so it's likely his own **Father**.
*   **Answer:** **His father**.

### Question 5
**Question:** "I have no brother or sister but that fellow's father is my father's son." Whose photo?
*   **Mental Trick:** No siblings → my father's son = me. That fellow's father = me. Photo = **his son**.
*   **Answer:** **His son**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Mr. Mehta's family with all listed relationships. How many members?
*   **Mental Trick:** 1 grandfather + 1 grandmother + their son + their daughter-in-law + 3 grandchildren (1 boy, 2 girls). That gives 7 people. Check all roles fit: ✅ (grandfather, grandmother = 1 couple; son + wife = 2nd couple; etc.)
*   **Answer:** **7 members**.

### Question 7
**Question:** + means mother of. – means brother. % means father. × means sister. P is maternal uncle of Q?
*   **Options:** `[A) Q-N+MxP  B) P+SxN-Q  C) P-M+NxQ  D) Q-S%P]`
*   **Mental Trick:** P is maternal uncle = P is brother of Q's mother. C: P-M means P is brother of M. M+N means M is mother of N. NxQ means N is sister of Q. So M is mother of N (and Q). P is M's brother = maternal uncle of Q ✅.
*   **Answer:** **C) P – M + N × Q**.

### Question 8
**Question:** "His mother is the wife of my father's son. No siblings." Whose portrait?
*   **Options:** `[A) Son  B) Cousin  C) Uncle  D) Nephew]`
*   **Mental Trick:** No siblings → father's son = me. His mother = my wife. He = my son.
*   **Answer:** **A) His son**.

### Question 9
**Question:** CO is daughter of TO's father's only brother OK. MO is only son of BO and brother of TO. PO is sister of OK. NO is mother of CO. BO is not aunt of CO. Sister of BO?
*   **Options:** `[A) CO  B) NO  C) TO  D) PO]`
*   **Mental Trick:** TO's father's only brother = OK. MO is son of BO and brother of TO → BO is mother of TO (and MO). CO is OK's daughter. NO is CO's mother = OK's wife. PO is OK's sister. BO is not aunt of CO → BO is not OK's sister. BO is TO's mother. Who is BO's sister? BO and PO could be sisters if they share parents. Actually OK and TO's father are brothers. PO is OK's sister → PO is also TO's father's sister. BO married TO's father. BO's sister? Not enough info. Answer: **D) PO**.
*   **Answer:** **D) PO**.

### Question 10
**Question:** "He is my mother's mother's only son." Woman's relation to man?
*   **Options:** `[A) Cousin  B) Mother  C) Niece  D) Aunt]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Mother's mother = grandmother. Grandmother's only son = grand-uncle (mother's uncle). Woman is his grand-niece. But from options: he's the woman's maternal grandfather's only son = grandmother's brother. Woman is his niece's daughter. Simplest: woman is grand-niece, nearest = **C) Niece**.
*   **Answer:** **C) Niece**.

### Question 11
**Question:** P × Q = sister. P + Q = father. P - Q = mother. S is aunt of T?
*   **Mental Trick:** Aunt = sister of parent. S × (someone) - T or S × (someone) + T. Check: S × X - T = S is sister of X, X is mother of T. So S is T's aunt (mother's sister) ✅. Without exact options visible, the answer follows the pattern.
*   **Answer:** **S × X - T** (S is sister of X who is mother of T).

### Question 14
**Question:** Her mother's brother is the only son of my mother's father. Girl's mother related to Amar?
*   **Options:** `[A) Mother  B) Sister  C) Aunt  D) Grandmother  E) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** My mother's father = grandfather. His only son = my mother's brother (uncle). Her mother's brother = my uncle. So her mother is my uncle's sister = my mother or aunt. **C) Aunt**.
*   **Answer:** **C) Aunt**.

### Question 15
**Question:** X is brother of son of Y's son. X related to Y?
*   **Options:** `[A) Son  B) Brother  C) Cousin  D) Grandson  E) Uncle]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Y's son's son = Y's grandson. Brother of grandson = also grandson. X = Y's grandson.
*   **Answer:** **D) Grandson**.

### Question 16
**Question:** "His only brother is the father of my daughter's father." Gentleman related to Deepak?
*   **Options:** `[A) Grandfather  B) Father  C) Brother-in-law  D) Uncle  E) Cannot determine]`
*   **Mental Trick:** My daughter's father = me (Deepak). Father of me = my father. Gentleman's only brother = Deepak's father. So gentleman is Deepak's father's brother = **D) Uncle**.
*   **Answer:** **D) Uncle**.

### Question 17
**Question:** D is grandmother of A, mother of B. C is wife of B, mother of F. F is granddaughter of E. Which is a couple?
*   **Options:** `[A) CD  B) DE  C) EB  D) Cannot determine  E) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** D is B's mother. C is B's wife → B&C are a couple. D is grandmother of A. F is granddaughter of E. E could be D's husband (grandfather) or C's father. If E = D's husband → D&E couple. B&C = couple. **B) DE** couple.
*   **Answer:** **B) DE**.

### Question 18
**Question:** M%N = son of. M@N = sister. M$N = father. C is granddaughter of E?
*   **Options:** `[A) C%B$F$E  B) B$F$E%C  C) C@B%F%E  D) E%B$F$C]`
*   **Mental Trick:** C is granddaughter → C is female child of E's child. C@B = C is sister of B. B%F = B is son of F. F%E = F is son of E. So C is sister of B, B is F's son, F is E's son → C is E's granddaughter ✅.
*   **Answer:** **C) C @ B % F % E**.

### Question 19

*   **Options:** `[A) Two  B) Three  C) Four  D) Cannot determine  E) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** D = grandmother (female). E = grandfather (male). B = D's son (male). C = B's wife (female). F = granddaughter (female). A = grandchild (gender unknown). Can't determine A's gender.
*   **Answer:** **D) Cannot be determined**.

### Question 20
**Question:** P+Q = brother. P-Q = mother. P*Q = sister. M is maternal uncle of R?
*   **Options:** `[A) M+K+R  B) M-R+K  C) M+K-R  D) M+K*R  E) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Maternal uncle = mother's brother. C: M+K = M is brother of K. K-R = K is mother of R. So M is brother of K who is mother of R. M is maternal uncle ✅.
*   **Answer:** **C) M + K - R**.
