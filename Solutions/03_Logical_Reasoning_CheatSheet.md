## 1. Blood Relations

### 📸 Pointing to a Photograph Trick
**Trick:** Start from the word **"MY"** and resolve backwards.
*   **Example Question:** Pointing to a man, a woman said: *"He is the son of the wife of the brother of MY father."* How is the man related to the woman?
    *   **Mental Solution:** Start at "MY father".
        1. "brother of (MY father)" -> Your **uncle**.
        2. "wife of (Your uncle)" -> Your **aunt**.
        3. "son of (Your aunt)" -> Your **cousin**.
        *Answer: The man is her cousin.*

## 2. Directions

### 🧭 Right/Left Turn Traps
*   **Right Turn** = Think **Clockwise**.
*   **Left Turn** = Think **Anti-Clockwise**.

### 📐 Shortest Distance (Pythagorean Triplets)
Memorize triplets: `3-4-5`, `5-12-13`, `8-15-17`.
*   **Example Question:** A man walks 3km North, turns right and walks 4km, then turns right again and walks exactly 3km. How far is he from the start?
    *   **Mental Solution:** Draw it mentally. Up 3, Right 4, Down 3. He forms a perfect rectangle! He is exactly **4km East** of his starting point. Wait, what if he didn't walk down 3?
*   **Alternate Example:** Walk 6km East, turn right (South) and walk 8km. How far from start?
    *   **Mental Solution:** This is a triangle with legs 6 and 8. Using the `3-4-5` triplet (multiplied by 2), the hypotenuse is `6-8-10`. Answer: **10km**.

## 3. Calendars

### 📅 The "Odd Days" Concept
Divide excess days by 7, remainder = "Odd Days".
*   **Example Question:** If 1st January 2023 is a Sunday, what day of the week is 1st January 2024?
    *   **Mental Solution:** 2023 is not a leap year. An ordinary year has exactly 1 "odd day". So the calendar shifts forward by 1 day. 
        *   Answer: **Monday**.
*   **Example Question 2:** Today is Tuesday. What day will it be after 65 days?
    *   **Mental Solution:** `65 / 7 = 9 weeks + 2 remainder`. Tuesday + 2 days = **Thursday**.

## 4. Clocks

### ⏱️ The Golden Clock Formula
**Angle = `| 30*H - 5.5*M |`**
*   **Example Question:** What is the exact angle between the hour and minute hands at 4:20?
    *   **Mental Solution:** H = 4, M = 20.
        *   `| 30*4 - 5.5*20 |` 
        *   `| 120 - 110 |` = **10 degrees**.
*   **Example Question 2:** At what time between 1 and 2 o'clock will the hands coincide (0 degrees)?
    *    **Mental Solution:** Use `Angle = 0`, `H = 1`. 
         *   `0 = 30(1) - 5.5(M)` -> `30 = 5.5M` -> `M = 300 / 55 = 60 / 11`.
         *   Answer: **1 hour, 5 and 5/11 minutes**.

## 5. Cubes (Painting & Cutting)

### 🎲 Painted Faces Formula
For an `n * n * n` cube cut from a larger painted cube:
*   3 faces (Corners) = **8**
*   2 faces (Edges) = `12 * (n - 2)`
*   1 face (Centers) = `6 * (n - 2)^2`
*   0 faces (Core) = `(n-2)^3`
*   **Example Question:** A solid cube painted red on all sides is cut into 27 smaller cubes. How many cubes have only one face painted?
    *   **Mental Solution:** 27 cubes means a `3 * 3 * 3` grid. So `n = 3`. 
    *   1 face painted = centers. Formula is `6 * (n-2)^2`.
    *   `6 * (3-2)^2` = `6 * 1` = **6 cubes**.

## 🔥 High-Frequency Exam Patterns & Hotspots

### 🕛 Clock Coincidence / Opposite Directions
*   Hands overlap (0 degrees) exactly **22 times** in 24 hours (not 24!). 
*   They form 180 degrees (opposite) exactly **22 times** in 24 hours. 
*   They form 90 degrees exactly **44 times** in 24 hours.

### 📅 Calendar Repetition Rule
A year's calendar repeats entirely after a fixed number of years:
*   **Leap year:** + 28 years.
*   **Leap year + 1:** + 6 years.
*   **Leap year + 2 or + 3:** + 11 years.

### ⭕ Circular Seating Maps
Almost every paper has a 6-to-8 person circular seating puzzle.
*   **Facing the Center:** *Right* = Anti-Clockwise, *Left* = Clockwise.
*   **Facing Outside:** *Right* = Clockwise, *Left* = Anti-Clockwise.
*   *Trick:* Always establish the "center" perspective before placing the first person. Draw a quick circle with arrows pointing in or out.
