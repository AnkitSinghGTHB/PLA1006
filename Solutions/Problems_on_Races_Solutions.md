# Problems on Races - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./02_Time_Speed_Work_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `PROBLEMS ON RACES.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Distance Ratio Rule:** In linear races, "A beats B by X meters" means that when A finishes the *entire* race, B is at `(Total - X)`. The ratio of their identical-time distances is the ONLY thing you need.

---

### Question 1
**Question:** Akshat runs 100m in 20s. Akshara runs 100m in 10s. By what distance will Akshara beat Akshat in a 100m race?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50 m  B) 25 m  C) 40 m  D) 60 m]`
*   **Mental Trick (Snapshot in Time):**
    *   Akshara finishes the 100m race in exactly 10 seconds.
    *   Where is Akshat at that exact 10-second mark?
    *   Akshat takes 20s to run 100m. This means in 10s (half the time), he covers exactly half the distance: 50m.
    *   Akshara is at 100m, Akshat is at 50m.
    *   Akshara beats him by `100 - 50 = 50m`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 50 m**.

### Question 2
**Question:** In a 100m race, A beats B by 10m and B beats C by 10m. Distance A beats C?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10 m  B) 20 m  C) 19 m  D) 11 m]`
*   **Mental Trick (Chaining Distance Ratios):**
    *   "A beats B by 10m" means when A is at 100, B is at 90. Ratio `A : B = 100 : 90 = 10 : 9`.
    *   "B beats C by 10m" means when B is at 100, C is at 90. Ratio `B : C = 100 : 90 = 10 : 9`.
    *   Chain them by making B equal! Multiply the first ratio by 10, the second by 9.
    *   `A : B : C = (10*10) : (9*10) : (9*9)` = **`100 : 90 : 81`**.
    *   When A runs exactly 100m, C has only run 81m.
    *   A beats C by `100 - 81 = 19m`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 19 m**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Sandeepa is 100% faster than Manisha. Sandeepa gives Manisha 200m head start. They finish simultaneously. Length of race?
*   **Options:** `[A) 200  B) 400  C) 300  D) 500]`
*   **Mental Trick (Ratio Gap):**
    *   "100% faster" means Sandeepa's speed is exactly **double** Manisha's.
    *   Since they finish simultaneously, Speed Ratio = Distance Ratio.
    *   Distance `S : M = 2 : 1`.
    *   This means Sandeepa runs exactly 1 part *more* than Manisha.
    *   This 1 part extra is the 200m head start!
    *   So, `1 part = 200m`.
    *   The total length of the race is Sandeepa's total distance (since she ran from the start line).
    *   Sandeepa = 2 parts = `2 * 200 = 400m`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 400**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Arun (30 m/s) and Barun (20 m/s) round 600m track. Participate in 3000m race. Distance covered by Arun when passing Barun for 5th time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2200 m  B) 2250 m  C) 2850 m  D) None of these]`
*   **Mental Trick (Logic Filter / Trick Question):**
    *   Calculate Arun's complete finish time: `3000m / 30 m/s = 100 seconds`. The race ENDS at 100 seconds!
    *   Now, calculate how long it takes to pass Barun ONCE:
    *   Relative speed = `30 - 20 = 10 m/s`.
    *   Time to gain 1 lap (600m) on Barun = `600 / 10 = 60 seconds`.
    *   Arun passes Barun for the **1st time** at 60 seconds.
    *   The **2nd time** would be at 120 seconds... but the race is already over at 100s!
    *   Since the 5th pass never happens during the race, the answer evaluates to none.
*   **Answer:** **D) None of these**.

### Question 10
**Question:** In 200m race, Amar beats Akbar by 20m, Anthony by 50m. In a 100m race, Akbar will beat Anthony by?
*   **Options:** `[A) 83.33 m  B) 16.66 m  C) 11.11 m  D) 88.89 m]`
*   **Mental Trick (Distance Snapshot):**
    *   When Amar crosses the 200m line:
        *   Akbar is at `200 - 20 = 180m`.
        *   Anthony is at `200 - 50 = 150m`.
    *   Ratio of `Akbar : Anthony` distances = `180 : 150` = `6 : 5`.
    *   In a 100m race, Akbar must cover 100m.
    *   Since Anthony covers `5/6` of whatever Akbar covers: `100 * (5/6) = 500/6 = 83.33m`.
    *   Akbar beats Anthony by `100 - 83.33 = 16.66m`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 16.66 m**.

### Question 11
**Question:** A beats B by 1 round or 10 min. They completed 4 rounds. A's time for one round?
*   **Options:** `[A) 8 min  B) 7.5 min  C) 12.5 min  D) 12 min]`
*   **Mental Trick (Missing Track Logic):**
    *   "A beats B by 1 round" means when A completes all 4 rounds, B has only completed 3 rounds in that exact same time!
    *   Let A's total time = `T`. This means B takes time `T` to run just 3 rounds.
    *   "A beats B by 10 minutes" means B needs 10 more minutes to finish his final 4th round.
    *   So, B runs 1 round in 10 minutes!
    *   If B takes 10 minutes per round, B's time for 3 rounds = 30 minutes.
    *   Since this matches A's total time (`T`), A takes 30 minutes to complete all 4 rounds!
    *   A's time for 1 round = `30 / 4 = 7.5 minutes`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 7.5 minutes**.
