# Work Equivalence - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./02_Time_Speed_Work_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Work Equivalence (TIME AND WORK).pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The `AND / OR` Formula:** A lifesaver for men/women problems! If a task is done by `X men OR Y women` in `D` days, the time taken by `A men AND B women` is exactly:
> `D / (A/X + B/Y)`

---

### Question 1
**Question:** 30 workers make 24 tables in 21 days working 8 hours/day. 35 workers want to make 18 tables in 12 days. How many hours/day?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 10  C) 7  D) 15]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Chain Rule Formula):**
    *   `(Men1 * Days1 * Hours1) / Work1 = (Men2 * Days2 * Hours2) / Work2`.
    *   `(30 * 21 * 8) / 24 = (35 * 12 * H) / 18`.
    *   Simplify LHS: `24 = 8 * 3`. The `8` cancels the top `8`. `21 / 3 = 7`. LHS is just `30 * 7 = 210`.
    *   Simplify RHS: `12` and `18` cancel to `2` and `3`. RHS is `(35 * 2 * H) / 3 = 70H / 3`.
    *   `210 = 70H / 3`.
    *   Divide both sides by 70: `3 = H / 3`.
    *   `H = 9`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 9**.

### Question 2
**Question:** 12 boys and 16 girls can do work in 10 days while 13 boys and 24 girls do it in 8 days. Time taken by 15 boys and 20 girls?
*   **Options:** `[A) 8  B) 10  C) 15  D) 4]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Multiplier Distractor):**
    *   Do NOT solve the giant simultaneous equations. Look closely at the groups.
    *   Group A: `12 boys + 16 girls` (Takes 10 days).
    *   Group Target: `15 boys + 20 girls`.
    *   Notice the ratio! The target group is exactly **1.25x** (or 5/4 times) larger than Group A.
    *   `(12 * 1.25 = 15)` and `(16 * 1.25 = 20)`.
    *   If the workforce is multiplied by `5/4`, the time taken must be multiplied by its inverse, `4/5`.
    *   `10 days * (4/5) = 8 days`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 8**.

### Question 4
**Question:** On 1st day 1 man works. On 2nd day 2 men. On nth day n men work. Work completes in 11 days. How many days for 11 men working together?
*   **Options:** `[A) 23  B) 11  C) 26  D) 6]`
*   **Mental Trick (Triangular Sum Factor):**
    *   Total work is simply the sum of the men working each day.
    *   `Work = 1 + 2 + 3 + ... + 11`.
    *   Sum of first 11 integers = `[n(n+1)] / 2` = `(11 * 12) / 2 = 66 units`.
    *   If 11 men work every day continuously, the rate is 11 units/day.
    *   Time = `66 / 11 = 6 days`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 6**.

### Question 7
**Question:** 20 men OR 24 women can complete a work in 20 days. How much time for 30 men AND 12 women?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10 days  B) 12 days  C) 15 days  D) 16 days]`
*   **Mental Trick (AND/OR Shortcut Formula):**
    *   Time = `(Given Days) / [ (Men_AND / Men_OR) + (Women_AND / Women_OR) ]`.
    *   Time = `20 / [ (30/20) + (12/24) ]`.
    *   Time = `20 / [ 1.5 + 0.5 ]`
    *   Time = `20 / 2 = 10 days`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 10 days**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Rajiv types 40 pages in 6 hours. Elango types 45 pages in 5 hours. Together typing 141 pages?
*   **Options:** `[A) 8 hours  B) 9 hours  C) 10 hours  D) 11 hours]`
*   **Mental Trick (Combined Rate):**
    *   Rajiv Rate = `40/6 = 20/3` pages per hour.
    *   Elango Rate = `45/5 = 9` pages per hour.
    *   Combined Rate = `20/3 + 9 = 20/3 + 27/3 = 47/3` pages per hour.
    *   Time = `Total Pages / Combined Rate` = `141 / (47/3)`.
    *   `141 * 3 / 47`. Notice that `47 * 3 = 141`!
    *   `3 * 3 = 9 hours`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 9 hours**.

### Question 10
**Question:** Raj + Shiva do work in 30 days. Raj works 16 days, Shiva finishes remaining in 44 days. Shiva alone for whole work?
*   **Options:** `[A) 30 days  B) 40 days  C) 60 days  D) 70 days]`
*   **Mental Trick (Overlap Breakdown):**
    *   Raj worked 16 days. Shiva worked 44 days.
    *   We can regroup this! Let's say `(Raj + Shiva) worked together for 16 days`, and then Shiva worked alone for the remaining `44 - 16 = 28 days`.
    *   Since they need 30 days total, working 16 days means they completed `16/30` of the work.
    *   Remaining work = `14/30`.
    *   Shiva does this `14/30` of the work in exactly 28 days.
    *   It literally takes Shiva 2 days to do just `1/30` of the work!
    *   So, for `30/30` (the whole thing), Shiva takes `30 * 2 = 60 days`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 60 days**.
