> [!TIP]
> **The `12/11` Trick:** Whenever you need to find the exact time hands coincide, are opposite, or at a specific angle, find the target minute position (e.g. 6 means 30) and multiply by `12/11`.

### Question 2
**Question:** What is the angle formed by the hour hand and the minute hand at 12:30 p.m.?
*   **Options:** `[a) 120  b) 135  c) 165  d) 180]`
*   **Mental Trick (Golden Formula):** `| 30*H - 5.5*M |`
    *   H = 12, M = 30.
    *   `| 30(12) - 5.5(30) |`
    *   `| 360 - 165 | = 195` degrees.
    *   Since 195 is the reflex angle, the inner angle is `360 - 195 = 165` degrees.
*   **Answer:** **c) 165 ⁰**.

### Question 3
**Question:** How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 24 hours?
*   **Options:** `[a) 20  b) 21  c) 22  d) 24]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Memorize clock frequencies! 
    *   Hands coincide 11 times every 12 hours (they skip the 12:00 overlap).
    *   Therefore, in 24 hours: `11 * 2 = 22 times`.
*   **Answer:** **c) 22**.

### Question 5
**Question:** At what time between 6 and 7 will the hour hand and minute hand coincide?
*   **Options:** `[a) 6:13  b) 6:36  c) 6:32 8/11  d) 6:35 8/11]`
*   **Mental Trick (The 12/11 shortcut):**
    *   If they coincide between 6 and 7, the minute hand must point at 6.
    *   The position of 6 on the clock is **30 minutes**.
    *   Multiply this base position by `12/11`.
    *   `30 * 12/11 = 360/11 = 32 8/11`.
*   **Answer:** **c) 6:32 8/11**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Dubai to London flight leaves Dubai at 10:00 a.m. London is 4 hours behind Dubai. Takes 9 hours to cover distance. Takes off 2 hours late. Local time reaching London?
*   **Options:** `[a) 7:00pm  b) 8:00pm  c) 5:00pm  d) 6:00pm]`
*   **Mental Trick (Single Timeline):** Convert to the destination time zone INSTANTLY to avoid confusion later.
    *   Dubai 10:00 a.m = London **6:00 a.m** (Since London is 4 hours behind).
    *   Flight duration = 9 hours. Expected landing time = `6:00 a.m. + 9 hours = 3:00 p.m. (London)`.
    *   It was late by 2 hours.
    *   Actual Landing = `3:00 p.m. + 2 hours = 5:00 p.m. (London)`.
*   **Answer:** **c) 5:00 p.m.**

### Question 7
**Question:** Wall clock slow by 10m. Table clock 10m ahead of wall clock. Alarm 5m behind table. Wrist 5m fast compared to alarm. All set right at Noon. What is Wrist Watch time at 6:00 p.m.?
*   **Options:** `[a) 6:00pm  b) 6:10pm  c) 8:00pm  d) 8:09pm]`
*   **Mental Trick (Net Deviation):** Map the fast/slow relative adjustments rapidly.
    *   Wall = `-10m` (from true time).
    *   Table = Wall `+ 10m` = `-10 + 10` = **0 (True Time!)**.
    *   Alarm = Table `- 5m` = `0 - 5` = `-5m`.
    *   Wrist = Alarm `+ 5m` = `-5 + 5` = **0 (True Time!)**.
    *   Since the wrist watch has zero net deviation, it shows the exact true time.
*   **Answer:** **a) 6:00 p.m.**

### Question 8
**Question:** At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will hands point in opposite directions?
*   **Options:** `[a) 54 min past 4  ...  d) 54 6/11 past 4]`
*   **Mental Trick (12/11 shortcut):**
    *   For opposite directions between 4 and 5, find the number exactly *opposite* to 4 on the clock face. It is **10**.
    *   Position of 10 is **50 minutes**.
    *   Multiply by `12/11`. `50 * 12/11 = 600/11 = 54 6/11`.
*   **Answer:** **d) 54 6/11 minutes past 4**.

### Question 12
**Question:** What time will a wall clock show after 1234567890 hours, if currently 11 a.m.?
*   **Options:** `[a) 11:00 am  b) 11:00 pm  c) 4:00 am  d) 5:00 am]`
*   **Mental Trick (Divisibility by 24):**
    *   You need to find the remainder of `1234567890 / 24`. 
    *   Instead of full division, divide by 24. A trick is identifying if it's a multiple of 12. `1234567800` is a multiple. Leaving `90`.
    *   `90 / 24` = 3 days with a remainder of **18 hours**.
    *   So, mathematically, you are just advancing the clock by 18 hours.
    *   `11 a.m. + 12 hours = 11 p.m.`
    *   `11 p.m. + 6 hours = 5 a.m.`
*   **Answer:** **d) 5:00 a.m.**

### Question 18
**Question:** A pendulum takes 7 seconds to strike 4 o’clock. Time (in sec) to strike 11 o’clock?
*   **Options:** `[a) 23.33  b) 20  c) 18  d) None]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Interval Trap):** 
    *   Striking 4 times means there are exactly **3 intervals** between the strikes (Strike1...Strike2...Strike3...Strike4).
    *   Time per interval = `7 / 3` seconds.
    *   Striking 11 times means there are **10 intervals**.
    *   Total Time = `10 * (7/3) = 70 / 3 = 23.33` seconds. 
*   **Answer:** **a) 23.33 seconds.**
