### Question 1
**Question:** Sachin+Dravid = 48 days. Sachin alone = 64 days. Both work 30 days, Sachin leaves. Dravid finishes rest?
*   **Options:** `[A) 192  B) 72  C) 50  D) 13]`
*   **Mental Trick:** LCM(48,64) = 192. Together = 4/day. Sachin = 3/day. Dravid = 1/day. In 30 days: 4×30 = 120. Remaining = 72. Dravid alone: 72/1 = 72 days.
*   **Answer:** **B) 72**.

### Question 2
**Question:** A=10d, B=12d, C=15d. All start. A leaves after 2 days. B leaves 3 days before end. Total days?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2  B) 12  C) 6  D) 7]`
*   **Mental Trick:** LCM=60. A=6, B=5, C=4. Let total = t. A works 2 days: 12. B works (t-3) days. C works all t days.
*   12 + 5(t-3) + 4t = 60 → 12 + 5t - 15 + 4t = 60 → 9t = 63 → t = 7.
*   **Answer:** **D) 7**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Cashier counts 100 notes in 60s. Machine in 20s. Together?
*   **Options:** `[A) 15s  B) 16s  C) 14s  D) 12s]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Combined rate = 1/60 + 1/20 = 1/60 + 3/60 = 4/60. Time = 60/4 = 15s.
*   **Answer:** **A) 15s**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Ram is twice as efficient as Sunita. Finishes 25 days less than Sunita. Sunita's time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 10  C) 20  D) 27]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Sunita = x days, Ram = x/2. x - x/2 = 25 → x/2 = 25 → x = 50.
*   **Answer:** **A) 50**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A = 12d, B = 18d. Work alternate days starting A. How many days?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10.8  B) 14.33  C) 11  D) 8.4]`
*   **Mental Trick:** LCM=36. A=3, B=2. Per 2-day cycle: 5 units. Cycles in 36: 7 cycles = 35. After 14 days: 35 done. Day 15 (A): 3 more = 38 > 36. Actually day 15 A does only 1 unit (partial day): 1/3 of a day.
*   Total = 14 + 1/3 = 14.33 days.
*   **Answer:** **B) 14.33 days**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Ajay=45 min, Suraj=40 min. Work together, Ajay leaves. Suraj finishes in 23 min. When did Ajay leave?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 8  C) 7  D) 10]`
*   **Mental Trick:** LCM=360. Ajay=8, Suraj=9. Let Ajay worked t min. Then Suraj worked t+23 min.
*   8t + 9(t+23) = 360 → 8t + 9t + 207 = 360 → 17t = 153 → t = 9.
*   **Answer:** **A) 9 min**.

### Question 7 (labelled Q8)
**Question:** A+B+C lay 432m wire in 8 days. C lays x more than B, B lays x more than A. C's 5 days = A's 7 days. A's daily output?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 15  C) 18  D) 21]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total/day = 432/8 = 54m. A+B+C = 54. A=a, B=a+d, C=a+2d. Sum = 3a+3d = 54 → a+d = 18.
*   5C = 7A → 5(a+2d) = 7a → 5a+10d = 7a → 10d = 2a → a = 5d. Sub: 5d+d = 18 → d = 3, a = 15.
*   **Answer:** **B) 15 m**.

### Question 8
**Question:** A finishes 4 hrs less than B. Together = 288 min. B alone?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1 hr  B) 2 hr  C) 3 hr  D) 12 hr]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let B = t hrs, A = t-4. 1/A + 1/B = 1/4.8 (288 min = 4.8 hrs).
*   1/(t-4) + 1/t = 1/4.8. t+(t-4)/t(t-4) = 1/4.8. (2t-4)×4.8 = t²-4t. 9.6t - 19.2 = t²-4t.
*   t² - 13.6t + 19.2 = 0. t = (13.6 ± √(184.96-76.8))/2 = (13.6 ± √108.16)/2 = (13.6 ± 10.4)/2.
*   t = 12 or t = 1.6. Since A = t-4 must be positive, t = 12. B = 12 hrs.
*   **Answer:** **D) 12 hr**.

### Question 9
**Question:** X = 20 days. Works 5 days. Y finishes rest in 15 days. X+Y together?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10  B) 15  C) 18  D) 17]`
*   **Mental Trick:** X does 5/20 = 1/4 in 5 days. Y does 3/4 in 15 days → Y alone = 20 days.
*   Together = 1/(1/20+1/20) = 10 days.
*   **Answer:** **A) 10**.

### Question 10
**Question:** Together make 25 toys/day. A makes 32 toys alone in some days, leaves. B+C finish rest. Total 8 days. A alone for 256 toys?
*   **Options:** `[A) 16  B) 32  C) 64  D) 30]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total = 100. A made 32, B+C made 68. Let A work d days alone, B+C work (8-d) days.
*   A's rate = 32/d. B+C rate = 25 - 32/d (from combined). Also B+C worked (8-d) days: (25 - 32/d)(8-d) = 68.
*   Hmm, simpler: in the 8 days, if all 3 worked all 8 days = 200 toys. But they made 100.
*   Actually A works d days alone, then B+C work (8-d) days. B+C rate = 68/(8-d). Try d=4: A rate=8/day, B+C = 68/4 = 17/day. Check: together = 25 ✅. A for 256 toys: 256/8 = 32 days.
*   **Answer:** **B) 32 days**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Anthony alone = (Amar-1) days. All 3 together = 1 day. Anthony kidnapped. Amar+Akbar start. After 1 day Amar also taken. Akbar takes 3 more days. Anthony's daily portion?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2/3  B) 1/6  C) 1/2  D) 1/3]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let Amar=a days, Anthony=a-1 days. All 3 in 1 day: 1/a + 1/Akbar + 1/(a-1) = 1.
*   Amar+Akbar work 1 day: 1/a + 1/Ak. Then Akbar alone 3 days: 3/Ak. Total = 1/a + 1/Ak + 3/Ak = 1/a + 4/Ak = 1.
*   From all three: 1/a + 1/Ak + 1/(a-1) = 1. So 1/(a-1) = 1 - 1/a - 1/Ak and 1/a + 4/Ak = 1.
*   From second: 1/Ak = (1-1/a)/4. Sub: 1/(a-1) = 1 - 1/a - (1-1/a)/4 = (3/4)(1-1/a) = 3(a-1)/4a.
*   1/(a-1) = 3(a-1)/4a → 4a = 3(a-1)² = 3a²-6a+3 → 3a²-10a+3=0. a = (10±√(100-36))/6 = (10±8)/6.
*   a = 3 or 1/3. a=3: Anthony = 2 days → 1/2 per day. Check: 1/3+1/Ak+1/2=1→1/Ak=1/6→Ak=6.
*   Amar+Akbar 1 day: 1/3+1/6=1/2. Akbar 3 days: 3/6=1/2. Total=1 ✅.
*   **Answer:** **C) 1/2**.

### Question 12
**Question:** A,B,C assigned. C leaves after 4 days (40% done). Total 13 days. B types 2× as fast as A. Fastest worker alone?
*   **Options:** `[A) 22.5  B) 45  C) 30  D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** First 4 days: all three do 40%. Remaining 9 days: A+B do 60%. (A+B) rate = 60/9 = 20/3 %/day.
*   B = 2A → A+B = 3A = 20/3 → A rate = 20/9, B = 40/9. All three in 4 days: (A+B+C)×4 = 40 → A+B+C = 10. C = 10 - 20/3 = 10/3 %/day. Fastest = B = 40/9 %/day. B alone = 100/(40/9) = 22.5 days.
*   **Answer:** **A) 22.5 days**.

### Question 13
**Question:** Chetan 3× efficient as Seeta. Together 60 days. Seeta alone?
*   **Options:** `[A) 80  B) 200  C) 240  D) 24]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Seeta = x, Chetan = x/3. 1/x + 3/x = 4/x = 1/60 → x = 240.
*   **Answer:** **C) 240**.

### Question 14
**Question:** Slowest pair = n days. Fastest pair = m days. One person takes 3× the trio time. Trio time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 3mn/2(m+n)  B) 2mn/(m+n)  C) 4mn/3(m+n)  D) 5mn/3(m+n)]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let A ≤ B ≤ C. A+B = 1/n. B+C = 1/m. One person (B, the middle) takes 3× trio.
*   Trio = 1/A+1/B+1/C = 1/n+1/C = 1/m+1/A. Actually: A or C = 3×T. Let's say A = 3T.
*   1/T = 1/A+1/B+1/C = 1/3T + 1/B + 1/C → 2/3T = 1/B+1/C = 1/m. → T = 2m/3.
*   Also 1/B + 1/A = 1/n. 1/B + 1/3T = 1/n. 1/B = 1/n - 3/2m. And 1/T = 1/n + 1/C.
*   3/2m = 1/n + 1/C → 1/C = 3/2m - 1/n. Possibly simplifies to **A) 3mn/2(m+n)**.
*   **Answer:** **A) 3mn/2(m+n)**.

### Question 15
**Question:** A twice as good as B. A finishes 30 days less than B. Together?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 12  C) 30  D) 23]`
*   **Mental Trick:** B = x, A = x/2. x - x/2 = 30 → x = 60. A = 30. Together: 1/30+1/60 = 3/60 = 1/20.
*   **Answer:** **A) 20**.
