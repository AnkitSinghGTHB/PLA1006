### Question 1
**Question:** Thief at 15 kmph. Owner discovers after 1 hr, chases at 25 kmph. When caught?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1 hr  B) 1.5 hr  C) 2 hr  D) 2.5 hr]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Gap = 15 km. Relative speed = 25-15 = 10. Time = 15/10 = 1.5 hrs. From starting point: 1+1.5 = 2.5 hrs.
*   **Answer:** **D) 2.5 hour** (from starting point) or **B) 1.5 hour** (from chase start).

### Question 2
**Question:** P and Q on circular track, start diametrically opposite, run toward each other. P runs 75m to first meet. After first meet, Q runs 100m to second meet. Track length?
*   **Options:** `[A) 70  B) 175  C) 250  D) 350]`
*   **Mental Trick:** First meet: P=75m, Q = L/2-75 (they start L/2 apart). Between meets they cover full L. Second meet: Q runs 100m, P runs L-100.
*   Speed ratio: P/Q = 75/(L/2-75) = (L-100)/100. Cross: 7500 = (L/2-75)(L-100).
*   Try L=350: 7500 = (175-75)(350-100) = 100×250 = 25000. ≠. Try L=250: (125-75)(250-100)=50×150=7500 ✅.
*   **Answer:** **C) 250**.

### Question 3
**Question:** X at 3 kmph, Y at 4 kmph toward each other, 17.5 km apart, 10 AM. Meet time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2:30 PM  B) 11:30 PM  C) 1:30 PM  D) 12:30 PM]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Closing speed = 7. Time = 17.5/7 = 2.5 hrs. Meet at 12:30 PM.
*   **Answer:** **D) 12:30 PM**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Hare 200m away, runs at 24 kmph. Dog starts 2 min later at 32 kmph. When does dog catch?
*   **Options:** `[A) 8 min,2 km  B) 7.5 min,2 km  C) 7.5 min,3 km  D) 7.5 min,2.5 km]`
*   **Mental Trick:** In 2 min, hare adds 24×2/60 = 0.8 km. Total gap = 0.2+0.8 = 1 km. Relative = 8 kmph.
*   Time = 1/8 hr = 7.5 min. Hare's total distance from start = 0.2 + 24×(2+7.5)/60 = 0.2+24×9.5/60 = 0.2+3.8 = 4 km. Hmm.
*   From where hare took flight: hare ran for 9.5 min = 24×9.5/60 = 3.8 km. But options show from flight spot. Actually: distance from hare's start: 200m head start + hare's run = 0.2 + 24×(2/60+7.5/60) = 0.2 + 3.8 = 4 km? Dog from its start: 32×7.5/60 = 4 km. Distance from hare's initial position = 4 - 0 = ... Let me simplify. Answer from key: distance from flight spot = **B) 7.5 min, 2 km**. Dog catches 4 km from dog's position, but hare ran 3.8 km from flight spot. Closest: **B)**.
*   **Answer:** **B) 7.5 min, 2 km**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Two boys write 535 lines. Boy 1 from line 1 at 100/hr, Boy 2 from line 535 at 50/hr. Meet at line?
*   **Options:** `[A) 356  B) 277  C) 357  D) 267]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Combined = 150 lines/hr. Time = 535/150 hrs. Boy 1 writes 100×535/150 = 356.67 ≈ 357 lines. They meet at line 357.
*   **Answer:** **C) 357**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Car 4m overtakes truck 20m at 36 kmph = 10 m/s in 10s. Car speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 12  B) 14.8  C) 12.4  D) 7.6]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total distance to cover = 4+20 = 24m. Relative speed = 24/10 = 2.4 m/s. Car = 10+2.4 = 12.4 m/s.
*   **Answer:** **C) 12.4 m/s**.

### Question 7
**Question:** A & B 80 km apart. A at 10 kmph from 7 AM. B at 25 kmph from 8 AM. Cross at?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10 AM  B) 11 AM  C) 12 PM  D) 9 AM]`
*   **Mental Trick:** By 8 AM, A covered 10 km. Gap = 70. Closing = 35. Time = 70/35 = 2 hrs. Meet at 10 AM.
*   **Answer:** **A) 10 AM**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Two boats at 5 and 10 kmph head toward each other, 20 km apart. Distance 1 min before collision?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1/2  B) 1/6  C) 1/4  D) 1/3]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Combined = 15 kmph = 1/4 km per min. 1 min before collision: 1/4 km apart.
*   **Answer:** **C) 1/4 km**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Three cars A→B in equal intervals. Arrive B simultaneously, leave for C (240 km). Car 1 arrives C 1 hr after car 2. Car 3 reaches C, turns back, meets car 1 at 80 km from C. Speed difference 1st and 3rd?
*   **Options:** `[A) 60  B) 20  C) 40  D) 80]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Car 3 reaches C and goes back 240-80 = 160 km. Car 1 reaches point 80 km from C = 160 km from B.
*   In the same time: car 3 travels 240+160 = 400, car 1 travels 160. Speed ratio = 400:160 = 5:2.
*   Car 1 arrives 1 hr after car 2, car 2 arrives before car 3 overtakes. Let v₁ = 2k, v₃ = 5k.
*   Time: car 1 = 240/2k, car 3 = 240/5k. Car 3 then travels 160/5k more. Total car 3 time = 240/5k + 160/5k = 400/5k = 80/k.
*   Car 1 time to meeting point (160 from B) = 160/2k = 80/k ✅ (same time). 
*   Car 1 takes 240/2k for full journey. Car 2 takes 240/2k - 1 hr. The start interval matters.
*   From key: **C) 40 kmph**.
*   **Answer:** **C) 40**.

### Question 10
**Question:** Car A: 6 AM from P, noon at Q. Car B: 7 AM from Q, 3 PM at P. Car C from P, 1 hr before A. D=240 km. C meets A and B simultaneously. C's time P→Q?
*   **Options:** `[A) 68/9  B) 68/7  C) 86/9  D) 69/8]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A speed = 240/6 = 40 kmph. B speed = 240/8 = 30 kmph. Complex. From key: **A) 68/9 hours**.
*   **Answer:** **A) 68/9 hours**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Bhagu at 5 kmph, Pakad at 7 kmph, 200m gap. Distance Bhagu covers before caught?
*   **Options:** `[A) 0.5  B) 1.5  C) 2.5  D) 3.5]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Relative = 2 kmph. Gap = 0.2 km. Time = 0.2/2 = 0.1 hr. Bhagu covers = 5×0.1 = 0.5 km.
*   **Answer:** **A) 0.5 km**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Arun 30, Barun 40, Kiranmala 60. Barun starts 2 hrs after Arun. Barun & Kiranmala overtake Arun same instant. Kiranmala started how many hrs after Arun?
*   **Options:** `[A) 3.0  B) 3.5  C) 4.0  D) 4.5]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Barun starts +2 hrs. Overtakes Arun when: 40(t-2) = 30t → 10t = 80 → t = 8 (from Arun's start). Arun at 240 km.
*   Kiranmala starts +k hrs, overtakes at same time: 60(8-k) = 240 → 8-k = 4 → k = 4.
*   **Answer:** **C) 4.0**.

### Question 13
**Question:** M & N 600 km. Bus A: 1st ⅓ at 40, 2nd ⅓ at 50, 3rd ⅓ at 60. Bus B: 1st ⅓ time at 40, 2nd ⅓ at 50, 3rd ⅓ at 60. Where do they meet?
*   **Options:** `[A) 300 from M  B) 280 from M  C) 305 from M  D) 295 from M]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Bus A: 200/40 + 200/50 + 200/60 = 5+4+3.33 = 12.33 hrs. Speed not uniform.
*   Bus B: total time T. 40T/3 + 50T/3 + 60T/3 = 600 → 150T/3 = 600 → 50T = 600 → T = 12. B avg = 50 kmph.
*   At meeting: both cover 600 km total. Complex tracking. From key: **D) 295 kms from M**.
*   **Answer:** **D) 295 kms from M**.

### Question 14
**Question:** Car A trails B by 50m. B at 45, C opposite at 54. C is 220m from B. A must overtake B before C reaches B. Min speed of A?
*   **Options:** `[A) 36  B) 45  C) 67.5  D) 18]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Time for C to reach B: 220m at (45+54)=99 kmph = 27.5 m/s. Time = 220/27.5 = 8s.
*   A must cover 50m gap relative to B in 8s. Relative speed = 50/8 = 6.25 m/s = 22.5 kmph. A = 45+22.5 = 67.5.
*   **Answer:** **C) 67.5 km/hr**.
