### Question 1
**Question:** CI on Rs. 8000 at 5% for 3 years, compounded yearly?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1262  B) 1261  C) 1260  D) 1361]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A = 8000 × (1.05)³. (1.05)² = 1.1025. (1.05)³ = 1.157625. A = 9261. CI = 9261 - 8000 = 1261.
*   **Answer:** **B) Rs. 1261**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Interest in 1st year = Rs. 400, 2nd year = Rs. 420. Find sum.
*   **Options:** `[A) 8000  B) 7500  C) 8500  D) 6500]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Extra interest in year 2 = 420 - 400 = 20. This is interest-on-interest = R% of 400. R = 20/400 = 5%. Principal = 400/0.05 = 8000.
*   **Answer:** **A) Rs. 8000**.

### Question 3
**Question:** $2000 becomes $2205 in 2 years CI. Rate?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2%  B) 3%  C) 4%  D) 5%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 2205/2000 = 1.1025 = (1 + R/100)². √1.1025 = 1.05. R = 5%.
*   **Answer:** **D) 5%**.

### Question 13
**Question:** Quarterly instalments of Rs. 17,576 each for 3 quarters. Rate = 16% p.a. compounded quarterly. Sum borrowed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 46900  B) 48775  C) 68320  D) 44556]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Quarterly rate = 4%. PV = 17576/1.04 + 17576/1.04² + 17576/1.04³.
*   = 17576 × (1/1.04 + 1/1.0816 + 1/1.1249) = 17576 × (0.9615 + 0.9246 + 0.8890)
*   = 17576 × 2.7751 ≈ 48,775.
*   **Answer:** **B) 48,775**.

### Question 15
**Question:** 40% for 2 years. CI half-yearly gives Rs. 568 more than CI yearly. Principal?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10000  B) 12000  C) 6000  D) 5000]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Yearly: (1.4)² = 1.96. Half-yearly (20% per half): (1.2)⁴ = 2.0736.
*   Difference multiplier = 2.0736 - 1.96 = 0.1136. P × 0.1136 = 568. P = 5000.
*   **Answer:** **D) 5000**.

### Question 16
**Question:** Population 5000, death rate 20%, no births. After 3 years?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1280  B) 3600  C) 2560  D) 2780]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Each year retains 80%. (0.8)³ = 0.512. Pop = 5000 × 0.512 = 2560.
*   **Answer:** **C) 2560**.

### Question 17

### Question 18
**Question:** 10% CI half-yearly. Deposits Rs. 1000 on Jan 1 and Jul 1. Interest at year end?
*   **Options:** `[A) 2152.50  B) 152.50  C) 1152.50  D) 315]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Jan 1 deposit: 2 half-years. Amount = 1000 × (1.05)² = 1102.50.
*   Jul 1 deposit: 1 half-year. Amount = 1000 × 1.05 = 1050.
*   Total = 2152.50. Interest = 2152.50 - 2000 = 152.50.
*   **Answer:** **B) Rs. 152.50**.

### Question 20
**Question:** Rs. 10,000 split between siblings aged 5 and 6. At 2% CI, both get equal at age 18. Younger one's share?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4000  B) 4950  C) 4735  D) 4335]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Younger (age 5) invests for 13 years. Elder (age 6) for 12 years.
*   Let younger get x. x × (1.02)¹³ = (10000 - x) × (1.02)¹².
*   Divide both sides by (1.02)¹²: x × 1.02 = 10000 - x → 1.02x + x = 10000 → 2.02x = 10000 → x = 4950.5.
*   **Answer:** **B) 4950**.
