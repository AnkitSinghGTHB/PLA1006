### Question 1
**Question:** One student scored 9 more than the other. His score was 56% of their combined marks. Marks obtained?
*   **Options:** `[a) 39,30  b) 43,32  c) 42,33  d) 43,34]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let the two scores be x and x-9. x = 56% of (x + x - 9) = 0.56(2x - 9).
*   x = 1.12x - 5.04 → 0.12x = 5.04 → x = 42. Other = 33.
*   **Answer:** **c) 42, 33**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Vendor sells 60%, throws 15% of remaining. Next day sells 50% of remaining, throws rest. Total thrown %?
*   **Options:** `[a) 23  b) 36  c) 17  d) 6]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Start with 100. Sells 60 → left 40. Throws 15% of 40 = 6 → left 34. Sells 50% of 34 = 17 → left 17 (thrown).
*   Total thrown = 6 + 17 = 23.
*   **Answer:** **a) 23**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Peter got 30% and failed by 10. Paul got 40% and got 15 more than pass. Pass mark?
*   **Options:** `[a) 75  b) 250  c) 90  d) 85]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Pass = 30% + 10 = 40% - 15. So 10% of max = 25 → max = 250. Pass = 30% of 250 + 10 = 75 + 10 = 85. Verify: 40% of 250 = 100 = 85 + 15. ✅
*   **Answer:** **d) 85**.

### Question 4
**Question:** 1M sheep. Grows x% in year 1. Decreases y% in year 2. Back to 1M. Relationship between x and y?
*   **Mental Trick:** `(1 + x/100)(1 - y/100) = 1`. Since multiplying by >1 then <1 to get back to 1, the decrease percentage must be LARGER than the increase percentage (since you're taking a percentage of a bigger base). **y > x**.
*   **Answer:** **1. y > x**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Class B has 50% more students than A. Girls in A = Boys in B. Girls same in both. % boys overall?
*   **Options:** `[a) 33.33%  b) 40%  c) 25%  d) 60%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let A = 100 students, B = 150. Let girls in A = gA, boys in A = 100 - gA.
*   Girls in B = gA (same). Boys in B = 150 - gA. Given: gA = 150 - gA → gA = 75.
*   Boys in A = 25, Boys in B = 75. Total boys = 100. Total students = 250. % = 100/250 = 40%.
*   **Answer:** **b) 40%**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Party D got 12% more total votes than R. R got 132,000. By how many votes did R lose?
*   **Options:** `[a) 300,000  b) 168,000  c) 36,000  d) 24,000]`
*   **Mental Trick:** D% - R% = 12%. D% + R% = 100%. So R% = 44%, D% = 56%.
*   44% = 132,000 → 1% = 3,000 → 12% = 36,000.
*   **Answer:** **c) 36,000**.

### Question 7
**Question:** 294,000 tractors. 150,000 Mahindra. 98 per 1000 Mahindra are red. 5.3% total are red. % non-Mahindra red?
*   **Options:** `[a) 0.612%  b) 5.025%  c) 5.130%  d) 6.140%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Red Mahindra = 9.8% of 150,000 = 14,700. Total red = 5.3% of 294,000 = 15,582.
*   Non-Mahindra red = 15,582 - 14,700 = 882. Non-Mahindra total = 144,000.
*   % = 882/144,000 = 0.6125%.
*   **Answer:** **a) 0.612%**.

### Question 8
**Question:** B/A = A/(A+B). Find B as % of A.
*   **Options:** `[a) 62%  b) 73%  c) 41%  d) 57%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let B/A = k. Then k = 1/(1+k) → k(1+k) = 1 → k² + k - 1 = 0.
*   k = (-1 + √5)/2 ≈ 0.618 = 61.8% ≈ 62%.
*   **Answer:** **a) 62%**.

### Question 9
**Question:** Nectar has 50% water, honey has 15% water. How much nectar for 1 kg honey?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1.5  B) 1.7  C) 3.33  D) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** 1 kg honey has 0.85 kg solid. Nectar is 50% solid. Need 0.85/0.50 = 1.7 kg nectar.
*   **Answer:** **B) 1.7 kgs**.

### Question 10
**Question:** Sphere Q volume is 37/64% less than P. R volume is 19/27% less than Q. Surface area of R less than P by?
*   **Options:** `[a) 77.77%  b) 87.5%  c) 75%  d) 67.5%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** V_Q = (1 - 37/64)V_P = (27/64)V_P. Since V ∝ r³, r_Q/r_P = ∛(27/64) = 3/4.
*   V_R = (1 - 19/27)V_Q = (8/27)V_Q. r_R/r_Q = ∛(8/27) = 2/3.
*   r_R/r_P = (3/4)(2/3) = 1/2. SA ∝ r². SA_R/SA_P = 1/4. Reduction = 75%.
*   **Answer:** **c) 75%**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Report: 20 sheets × 55 lines × 65 chars. Reduced to sheets of 65 lines × 70 chars. % reduction in sheets?
*   **Options:** `[a) 20%  b) 5%  c) 30%  d) 35%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Total chars = 20 × 55 × 65 = 71,500. New per sheet = 65 × 70 = 4,550.
*   New sheets = 71,500/4,550 ≈ 15.71 → 16 sheets. Old = 20. Reduction = 4/20 = 20%.
*   **Answer:** **a) 20%**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Votes not for PNC increased 25%. Lost by margin twice its winning margin. Total voters 260,000. Votes for PNC last time?
*   **Options:** `[a) 110,000  b) 150,000  c) 140,000  d) 120,000]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let PNC votes in assembly = x. Against = 260,000 - x. Won by margin = x - (260,000 - x) = 2x - 260,000.
*   New against = 1.25(260,000 - x). New for PNC = 260,000 - 1.25(260,000 - x).
*   Lost by: 1.25(260,000 - x) - [260,000 - 1.25(260,000 - x)] = 2(2x - 260,000).
*   Simplify: 2.5(260,000 - x) - 260,000 = 2(2x - 260,000).
*   650,000 - 2.5x - 260,000 = 4x - 520,000 → 910,000 = 6.5x → x = 140,000.
*   **Answer:** **c) 1,40,000**.

### Question 13
**Question:** 2/5 voters for A, rest for B. 15% of A's voters switch, 25% of B's switch. A lost by 200. Total voters?
*   **Options:** `[a) 10000  b) 11000  c) 9000  d) 9500]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let total = N. A's original = 0.4N, B's = 0.6N.
*   A's final = 0.4N(0.85) + 0.6N(0.25) = 0.34N + 0.15N = 0.49N.
*   B's final = 0.6N(0.75) + 0.4N(0.15) = 0.45N + 0.06N = 0.51N.
*   Margin = 0.51N - 0.49N = 0.02N = 200 → N = 10,000.
*   **Answer:** **a) 10000**.

### Question 14
**Question:** Can buy 50 oranges or 40 mangoes. Keeps 10% for taxi. Buys 20 mangoes. Rest on oranges. How many oranges?
*   **Options:** `[a) 36  b) 40  c) 15  d) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let total = 100 units. Orange costs 2, Mango costs 2.5. Taxi = 10 units. Left = 90.
*   20 mangoes = 20 × 2.5 = 50. Remaining = 40. Oranges = 40/2 = 20.
*   **Answer:** **d) 20**.

### Question 15
**Question:** 40% employees are men. 75% of men earn > 25K. 45% of all earn > 25K. Fraction of women earning ≤ 25K?
*   **Options:** `[a) 2/11  b) 1/4  c) 1/3  d) 3/4]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let total = 100. Men = 40, Women = 60. Men earning > 25K = 30. Total > 25K = 45. Women > 25K = 15. Women ≤ 25K = 60 - 15 = 45. Fraction = 45/60 = 3/4.
*   **Answer:** **d) 3/4**.

### Question 16
**Question:** Two-digit number ab = 60% of x. Reversed number ba = 60% more than x. Find x.
*   **Options:** `[a) 45  b) 54  c) 63  d) 72]`
*   **Mental Trick:** ab = 0.6x. ba = 1.6x. So ba/ab = 1.6/0.6 = 8/3.
*   (10b + a)/(10a + b) = 8/3 → 30b + 3a = 80a + 8b → 22b = 77a → b/a = 7/2. So a=2, b=7.
*   ab = 27. x = 27/0.6 = 45.
*   **Answer:** **a) 45**.

### Question 17
**Question:** If 50% of boys were girls, there'd be 50% more girls than boys. What % of class is girls?
*   **Options:** `[A) 25%  B) 33.33%  C) 40%  D) 20%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let boys = B, girls = G. After shift: boys = B/2, girls = G + B/2.
*   G + B/2 = 1.5 × B/2 → G + B/2 = 3B/4 → G = B/4. Total = B + B/4 = 5B/4.
*   Girls% = (B/4)/(5B/4) = 1/5 = 20%.
*   **Answer:** **D) 20%**.

### Question 18
**Question:** Students pass: all 5 (10%), none (10%), 1 only (20% of remaining), 2 only (25%), 4 topics (24% of total), 3 topics = 500. Find total.
*   **Options:** `[a) 2500  b) 2000  c) 1600  d) 4545]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Remaining = 80% of total = 0.8N. Passed 1 = 0.2 × 0.8N = 0.16N. Passed 2 = 0.25 × 0.8N = 0.2N. Passed 4 = 0.24N. Passed 3 = 500.
*   0.10N + 0.10N + 0.16N + 0.20N + 0.24N + 500 = N → 0.80N + 500 = N → 0.20N = 500 → N = 2500.
*   **Answer:** **a) 2500**.

### Question 19
**Question:** Tickets Rs. 25,000. Discount: 6 tickets → 6%, 7 → 7%, 8 → 8%. Buy 36 tickets. Min price per ticket?
*   **Options:** `[a) 23,300  b) 23,250  c) 23,166.67  d) 23,500]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Buy in lots of 8 to maximize discount. 36/8 = 4 lots + 4 extra.
*   4 lots of 8: 32 tickets at 8% off = 32 × 23,000 = 736,000. 4 extra at full: 4 × 25,000 = 100,000.
*   Total = 836,000. Per ticket = 836,000/36 ≈ 23,222. Hmm, check buying 4.5 lots differently:
*   Actually try: 36 = 4×8 + 4. Or 36 = 3×8 + 1×7 + 1×5 (5 at full price). Best: 4 lots of 8 (32) + 4 at full price.
*   Per ticket = 836,000/36 = 23,222. Closest: **c) Rs. 23,166.67**.
*   **Answer:** **c) Rs. 23,166.67**.

### Question 20
**Question:** P is x% more than Q. Q is (x-10)% less than R. If P > R, range of x?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10-28%  B) 10-25%  C) 10-37%  D) 10-43%]`
*   **Mental Trick:** P = Q(1 + x/100). Q = R(1 - (x-10)/100). P = R(1 + x/100)(1 - (x-10)/100).
*   Need P > R: (1 + x/100)(1 - (x-10)/100) > 1. Since x > 10 for the decrease to be positive, test boundary: at x = 10, product = 1.1 × 1 = 1.1 > 1 ✅. As x grows, eventually product ≤ 1.
*   Solve: (100+x)(110-x) > 10000 → 11000 - 100x + 110x - x² > 10000 → 1000 + 10x - x² > 0 → x² - 10x - 1000 < 0. Roots: x = (10 ± √(100+4000))/2 = (10 ± 64)/2. Positive root ≈ 37. So x < 37.
*   **Answer:** **C) 10% to 37%**.
