### Question 1
**Question:** A→B(2km), right→C(3km), right→D(8km), right→E(3km), right→F(4km). Distance A to F?
*   **Mental Trick:** Draw on grid. A(0,0)→B(0,2)north→C(3,2)east→D(3,-6)south→E(0,-6)west→F(0,-2)north. F=(0,-2). AF = 2 km south of A. Distance = 2 km.
*   **Answer:** **2 km**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Bus from E goes 31m north, honks once (left), drives 35m. To reach X, drive ____?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5  B) 10  C) 15  D) 5√2]`
*   **Mental Trick:** From the coordinate system: J=20m east of B. C=33m south of J. C=15m west of D. D midpoint of EF (vertical). E=30m north of F (so D is 15m above F). F=40m east of G. H=24m north of G. K=10m east of H. Gate X=18m north of B.
*   Set B=(0,0). J=(20,0). C=(20,-33). D=(35,-33). E=(35,-18), F=(35,-48). G=(-5,-48). H=(-5,-24). K=(5,-24). X=(0,18).
*   Bus from E(35,-18) goes 31m north → (35,13). Honks once = left turn (now facing west) → goes 35m west → (0,13). To reach X(0,18): go 5m north.
*   **Answer:** **A) 5 m**.

### Question 3
**Question:** 40m N, left 40m W, left 15m S, right 20m W. Distance and direction from start?
*   **Options:** `[A) 55m NW  B) 36m NE  C) 65m SE  D) 65m NW  E) None]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Start(0,0). N40→(0,40). W40→(-40,40). S15→(-40,25). W20→(-60,25).

*   **Answer:** **D) 65m, Northwest**.

*   **Answer:** **D) South**.

### Question 25
**Question:** Ravi 20km N of Sunny. Sunny 18km E of Suraj, 12km W of Mahi. Amitabh 15km S of Sunny. Sunny meets Mahi then Ravi. Distance?

Sunny to Mahi=12. Mahi position: if Sunny=(0,0), Mahi=(12,0), Ravi=(0,20). Mahi to Ravi = √(144+400) ≈ 23.3. Total ≈ 35.3. But question asks Sunny meeting Ravi AFTER meeting Mahi: 12+23.3 ≈ 35.
*   **Answer:** **B) 35** (approximately).
