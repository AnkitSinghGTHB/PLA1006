# Mixtures & Alligations - Mental Math Solutions

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Mixtures & Alligations.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Part-Locking Trick:** When one component of a mixture is added, the *other* component stays constant. Always lock the constant component's parts and evaluate the change in the added component's parts!

### Question 1
**Question:** Mixture is 56 litres, Acid to Base is 5:2. How much base to add to make it 5:3?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10 lts  B) 4 lts  C) 8 lts  D) 15 lts]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Examine the ratios: `5:2` becomes `5:3`.
    *   The Acid is already locked at 5 parts. Perfect! 
    *   The Base goes from 2 parts to 3 parts. That is a change of exactly **+1 part**.
    *   What is the value of 1 part? 
    *   Initial mixture is `5 + 2 = 7 parts`.
    *   `7 parts = 56 litres`  →  `1 part = 8 litres`.
    *   Since we need to add 1 part, we add 8 litres.
*   **Answer:** **C) 8 lts**.

### Question 2 (Listed as 100kg Ammonium problem)
**Question:** Ratio of Sodium Chloride to Ammonium in 100kg mixture is 7:27. Amount of Sodium Chloride to add to make ratio 10:27?
*   **Options:** `[A) 6.7 kg  B) 8.8 kg  C) 9 kg  D) 11.1 kg]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Ratios: `7:27` to `10:27`.
    *   Ammonium is locked at 27. Perfect!
    *   Sodium Chloride changes from 7 to 10 parts. Change is **+3 parts**.
    *   Initial mixture = `7 + 27 = 34 parts`.
    *   `34 parts = 100 kg`.
    *   `1 part = 100 / 34 kg`.
    *   `3 parts = 300 / 34 = 150 / 17`.
    *   Mentally: `17 * 9 = 153`. So it's slightly less than 9. `8.8` fits perfectly.
*   **Answer:** **B) 8.8 kg**.

### Question: Alloy weighing 21-gm
**Question:** Alloy has Zinc:Copper = 2:5 (21gm). If 30gm Zinc is added, what amount of Copper must be REMOVED so new ratio is 6:1?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5 gm  B) 9 gm  C) 15 gm  D) 18 gm]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Find initial weights: `2+5=7 parts = 21gm`. So `1 part = 3gm`.
    *   Zinc = `6gm`, Copper = `15gm`.
    *   Add 30gm Zinc. New Zinc = `6 + 30 = 36gm`.
    *   New Target Ratio is **6:1** (Zinc:Copper).
    *   Since Zinc is 6 parts, and weighs 36gm, then `1 part = 6gm`.
    *   This means our Copper *must* be 6gm.
    *   We currently have 15gm. We need it to be 6gm.
    *   Remove `15 - 6 = 9gm`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 9 gm**.

### Question 7 (Three Bottles)
**Question:** Three bottles contain equal mixtures. Ratios are 4:1, 3:2, and 2:1. When mixed together, final ratio?
*   **Options:** `[A) 15:17  B) 23:27  C) 31:14  D) 12:15]`
*   **Mental Trick (LCM Balancing):**
    *   Sum of parts for each bottle: `Bottle 1 = 5`, `Bottle 2 = 5`, `Bottle 3 = 3`.
    *   The bottles are "equal" volume, so we must make their sums identical. LCM of 5, 5, 3 is **15**.
    *   Multiply Bottle 1 scale by 3: `12 : 3`.
    *   Multiply Bottle 2 scale by 3: `9 : 6`.
    *   Multiply Bottle 3 scale by 5: `10 : 5`.
    *   Now just add vertically!
    *   Spirit = `12 + 9 + 10 = 31`.
    *   Water = `3 + 6 + 5 = 14`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 31:14**.

### Question 10 (Benzene concentration)
**Question:** One liquid has 24% benzene, other has 36%. Filled with 5 parts of first, 7 parts of second. Percentage in new mixture?
*   **Options:** `[A) 30%  B) 31%  C) 34%  D) 35%]`
*   **Mental Trick (Base Deviation):**
    *   Base percentage is 24%.
    *   The second liquid is `12%` richer (36 - 24 = 12).
    *   You have 7 parts of this richer liquid, spread across a total of 12 parts (`5 + 7 = 12`).
    *   Net shift = `+12% * (7/12) = +7%`.
    *   New mixture = `24% + 7% = 31%`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 31%**.

### Question 13 (Repeated Replacement)
**Question:** Milkman had 20L pure milk. Removed some milk, added equal water. Repeated again. Milk is now 64% pure. Amount removed initially?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4 litres  B) 6 litres  C) 6.6 litres  D) 5 litres]`
*   **Mental Trick (Fractional Roots):**
    *   The process happened **2 times**.
    *   Final pure concentration = 64%. Or `64 / 100 = 16 / 25`.
    *   Since it happened twice, take the **Square Root** of the fraction!
    *   `√ (16/25) = 4 / 5`.
    *   This means after ONE operation, only `4/5` of the milk remained.
    *   So, `1/5` of the total volume was removed.
    *   `1/5` of 20L = **4 litres**.
*   **Answer:** **A) 4 litres**.
