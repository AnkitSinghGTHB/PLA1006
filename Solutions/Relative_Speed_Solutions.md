# Relative Speed - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./02_Time_Speed_Work_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `RELATIVE SPEED.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The `One Minute Before` Trick:** If two objects are about to collide, the distance between them $T$ minutes before collision is ALWAYS just their Relative Speed multiplied by $T$. The initial starting distance is a complete distractor!

---

### Question 1
**Question:** Thief drives 15 km/hr. Discovered 1 hour later, owner chases at 25 km/hr. When will owner overtake?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1 hour  B) 1.5 hour  C) 2 hour  D) 2.5 hour]`
*   **Mental Trick (Gap Logic):**
    *   Initial gap = distance thief traveled in 1 hour = `15 km`.
    *   Relative speed (how fast the gap closes) = `25 - 15 = 10 km/hr`.
    *   Time to close gap = `15 / 10 = 1.5 hours`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 1.5 hour**.

### Question 2
**Question:** P and Q run in opposite directions on circular track from diametrically opposite points. First meet after P runs 75m. Second meet after Q runs 100m from first meeting. Track length?
*   **Options:** `[A) 70  B) 175  C) 250  D) 350]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Meeting Multiplier):**
    *   They start diametrically opposite, so the First Meeting happens after they collectively run EXACTLY Half the Circumference `(C/2)`.
    *   In this `C/2` distance, P ran 75m.
    *   The Second Meeting requires them to collectively run a FULL Circumference `(C)`.
    *   Since `C` is exactly double of `C/2`, the distance covered by each person will be DOUBLE what they covered initially!
    *   So, between 1st and 2nd meeting, P runs `75 * 2 = 150m`.
    *   Q ran `100m` between the meetings (given).
    *   The full circumference `C` is simply the sum of what they BOTH ran between the two meetings!
    *   `C = 150 (P's part) + 100 (Q's part) = 250m`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 250**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Booklet has 535 lines. Boy 1 writes fwd at 100 lines/hr. Boy 2 writes bwd at 50 lines/hr. At what line will they meet?
*   **Options:** `[A) 356  B) 277  C) 357  D) 267]`
*   **Mental Trick (Ratio Split):**
    *   Speed ratio Boy 1 : Boy 2 = `100 : 50` = `2 : 1`.
    *   This means Boy 1 writes exactly 2 lines for every 1 line Boy 2 writes.
    *   Split the total 535 lines into this `2:1` ratio.
    *   Boy 1's share = `(2/3) * 535 = 356.66` lines.
    *   Since lines are discrete integers, Boy 1 finishes line 356, Boy 2 finishes line 178 (total 534). They then both reach line `357` at the exact same moment.
*   **Answer:** **C) 357**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Car (4m) overtakes truck (20m) traveling at 36km/hr within 10 seconds. Car speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 12 m/s  B) 14.8 m/s  C) 12.4 m/s  D) 7.6 m/s]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Overtaking Distance = `Car Length + Truck Length` = `4 + 20 = 24m`.
    *   Relative Speed needed to cover 24m in 10s = `24 / 10 = 2.4 m/s`.
    *   Truck speed = `36 km/hr = 36 * (5/18) = 10 m/s`.
    *   Since the car is overtaking, its speed must be `Truck + Relative Speed`.
    *   `10 + 2.4 = 12.4 m/s`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 12.4 m/s**.

### Question 8
**Question:** Boats travel at 5 kmph and 10 kmph towards each other. Began 20km apart. How far apart ONE MINUTE before collision?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1/2  B) 1/6  C) 1/4  D) 1/3]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Collision Distractor):**
    *   Ignore the 20km entirely! It does not matter where they started or how long they drove.
    *   One minute before collision, the distance between them is exactly the distance they can collectively close in one minute.
    *   Relative Speed = `5 + 10 = 15 kmph`.
    *   Distance in 1 minute = `15 km/hr * (1/60 hr) = 15/60 km`.
    *   `15/60 = 1/4 km`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 1/4**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Cop spots thief from 200m. Thief runs 5kmph, Cop 7kmph. Distance thief covers before caught?
*   **Options:** `[A) 0.5 km  B) 1.5 km  C) 2.5 km  D) 3.5 km]`
*   **Mental Trick (Distance Gap Ratio):**
    *   Speed Ratio = `5 : 7`.
    *   Distance Ratio is identical because time is the same. Let's say `5 parts` and `7 parts`.
    *   The Cop runs `2 extra parts` than the thief.
    *   This extra distance is exactly the initial 200m gap!
    *   `2 parts = 200m` -> `1 part = 100m`.
    *   Thief's distance = `5 parts = 500m = 0.5 km`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 0.5 km**.

### Question 15
**Question:** Arun (30mph), Barun (40mph), Kiranmala (60mph). Barun starts 2 hrs after Arun. Barun & Kiranmala overtake Arun at the EXACT same instant. Kiranmala's start time after Arun?
*   **Options:** `[A) 3.0  B) 3.5  C) 4.0  D) 4.5]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Synchronization Point):**
    *   **Find the Meeting Point:**
        *   Arun has a 2-hour head start. Distance = `30 * 2 = 60 km`.
        *   Barun's Relative Speed vs Arun = `40 - 30 = 10 kmph`.
        *   Time for Barun to catch Arun = `Gap / Relative Speed` = `60 / 10 = 6 hours`.
    *   **Trace the Distance:**
        *   If Barun catches Arun after 6 hours of driving, Arun has been driving for `2 + 6 = 8 hours`.
        *   Distance from start line = `Arun's Speed * Total Time` = `30 * 8 = 240 km`.
    *   **Calculate Kiranmala:**
        *   Kiranmala reaches this exact 240 km mark at the exact same instant!
        *   Time she needed to drive 240km at 60 kmph = `240 / 60 = 4 hours`.
        *   Since the meeting happened at hour 8 (Arun's time), and she only drove for 4 hours, she must have started `8 - 4 = 4 hours` after Arun.
*   **Answer:** **C) 4.0**.
