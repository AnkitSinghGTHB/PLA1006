### Set 1 (Questions 1-5)
**Context:** Six athletes Anu, Benny, Chandru, Dolly, Esha, Furpreet compete in a race. The outcomes are as follows : 1) Exactly 2 swimmers finish ahead of Benny. 2) Only two swimmers separate Esha & Dolly. 3) Anu is behind Dolly & Esha. 4) Benny is ahead of Esha, with one swimmer intervening. 5) Furpreet is ahead of Dolly.
*   **Mental Trick (Arrangement):** 2 ahead of Benny → Benny = 3rd. Benny ahead of Esha, 1 between → Esha = 5th. 2 swimmers separate E & D → Dolly = 2nd. Anu behind both D(2) & E(5) → Anu = 6th. Furpreet ahead of D(2) → F=1. Chandru = remaining = 4th.
*   **Order:** Furpreet, Dolly, Benny, Chandru, Esha, Anu.

#### Question 1
**Question:** Who stood third in the race?
*   **Options:** `[A) Anu  B) Benny  C) Chandru  D) Dolly  E) Esha]`
*   **Answer:** **B) Benny**.

#### Question 2
**Question:** How many swimmers separate Furpreet and Benny?
*   **Options:** `[A) One  B) Two  C) Three  D) Four  E) Zero]`
*   **Answer:** **A) One**.

#### Question 3
**Question:** Who secured the middle position in the race?
*   **Options:** `[A) Anu  B) Benny  C) Chandru  D) Dolly  E) None of the options]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Positions 3 and 4 are the middle. The options ask for one person. Chandru is 4th.
*   **Answer:** **C) Chandru**.

#### Question 4
**Question:** Who wins the race?
*   **Options:** `[A) Anu  B) Furpreet  C) Chandru  D) Dolly  E) Esha]`
*   **Answer:** **B) Furpreet**.

#### Question 5
**Question:** How many swimmers finished ahead of Chandru?
*   **Options:** `[A) One  B) Two  C) Three  D) Four  E) None of the options]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Chandru is 4th, so 3 swimmers finished ahead of him.
*   **Answer:** **C) Three**.

### Set 2 (Questions 6-10)
**Context:** Priya, Qureshi, Ravi, Seema, Tina, Upasna, Vijay and Wahadutt are sitting in a circular table and are facing the center. 1) Priya is second to the right of Tina, who is the neighbour of Ravi and Vijay. 2) Seema is not the neighbour of Priya. 3) Vijay is the neighbour of Upasna. 4) Qureshi is not between Seema and Wahadutt. 5) Wahadutt is not between Upasna and Seema.
*   **Mental Trick (Arrangement):** Tina between Ravi & Vijay. Priya 2 seats right of Tina. Vijay next to Upasna. 
*   **Order (clockwise):** Ravi - Tina - Vijay - Upasna - Wahadutt - Seema - Qureshi - Priya.

#### Question 6
**Question:** Which of the following options represents neighbours?
*   **Options:** `[A) Ravi & Vijay  B) Upasna & Tina  C) Ravi & Priya  D) Upasna & Wahadutt]`
*   **Answer:** **D) Upasna & Wahadutt**.

#### Question 7
**Question:** Who is to the immediate right of Upasna?
*   **Options:** `[A) Priya  B) Vijay  C) Ravi  D) Tina]`
*   **Answer:** **B) Vijay** (looking towards center, depending on clockwise/anti-clockwise derivation; based on standard relative pos).

#### Question 8
**Question:** Which of the following is true?
*   **Options:** `[A) Priya is to the immediate right of Qureshi. B) Ravi is between Upasna and Vijay. C) Qureshi is to the immediate left of Wahadutt. D) Upasna is between Wahadutt and Seema.]`
*   **Answer:** **C) Qureshi is to the immediate left of Wahadutt** (or A depending on orientation, C fits the derived arrangement standard).

#### Question 9
**Question:** What is the position of Vijay?
*   **Options:** `[A) Between Upasna and Tina. B) Second to the right of Priya. C) To the immediate right of Wahadutt. D) Data inadequate.]`
*   **Answer:** **A) Between Upasna and Tina**.

#### Question 10
**Question:** Which of the following pair is sitting adjacent to each other?
*   **Options:** `[A) Qureshi & Vijay  B) Tina & Seema  C) Priya & Ravi  D) Upasna & Wahadutt]`
*   **Answer:** **D) Upasna & Wahadutt**.

### Set 4 (Questions 13-14)
**Context:** There are five different buildings. Following are the clues given: 1) A to E in a row. 2) A is to the right of B. 3) E is to the left of C and right of A. 4) B is to the right of D.
*   **Mental Trick (Arrangement):** D - B - A - E - C.

#### Question 13
**Question:** Which building is at extreme right end?
*   **Options:** `[A) A  B) C  C) D  D) E]`
*   **Answer:** **B) C**.

#### Question 14
**Question:** How many buildings are there between D and C?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 4]`
*   **Answer:** **C) 3**.

### Question 15
**Question:** Five letters are arranged in a row. 1. R is not adjacent to S or A. 2. B is not adjacent to S. 3. R is adjacent to M. 4. M is at the middle in the row. Then, B is adjacent to whom out of the following?
*   **Options:** `[A) R  B) S  C) A  D) Cannot be determined]`
*   **Mental Trick:** M is in the middle (Pos 3). R is adjacent to M (Pos 2 or 4). R not adjacent to S or A, so R is on an edge. Thus S and A are on the other side of M. B is not adjacent to S, meaning B must be next to A. Therefore B is adjacent to A.
*   **Answer:** **C) A**.

### Set 5 (Questions 16-22)
**Context:** Seven friends A, B, C, D, E, F and G studied in colleges X, Y and Z and are currently in different professions... At least two and not more than three friends had studied in the same college. C is an architect and studied in college Y. E is not a businessman. Only G amongst the seven friends studied in college X along with E. F is an engineer and did not study in college Y...
*   **Mental Trick (Arrangement):** College X has G & E. College Y has C(Architect), A, B. College Z has F(Engineer), D. Professions derived by elimination.

#### Question 16

#### Question 18

### Set 6 (Questions 23-25)
**Context:** Eleven friends M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V and W are sitting in the first row of the stadium watching a cricket match. T is to the immediate left of P and third to the right of U. V is the immediate neighbour of M and N and third to the left of S. M is the second to the right of Q, who is at one of the ends...
*   **Mental Trick (Arrangement):** Q is at one end. Solve left-to-right: Q - W - M - V - N - S - U - O - T - P - R.

#### Question 23
**Question:** Who is sitting in the center of the row?
*   **Options:** `[a) N  b) O  c) S  d) U]`
*   **Answer:** **c) S**.

#### Question 24
**Question:** Which of the following people are sitting to the right of S?
*   **Options:** `[a) OTPQ  b) OTPR  c) UNVM  d) UOTPR]`
*   **Answer:** **d) UOTPR**.

#### Question 25
**Question:** Which of the following statements is true with respect to the above arrangement?
*   **Options:** `[a) There are three persons sitting between P and S  b) W is between M and V.  c) N is sitting between V and U  d) S and O are neighbours...]`
*   **Answer:** **a) There are three persons sitting between P and S**.
