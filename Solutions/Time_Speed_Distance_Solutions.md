# Time, Speed and Distance - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./02_Time_Speed_Work_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `TIME, SPEED AND DISTANCE.pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Proportionality Flip:** Speed and Time are inversely proportional! If your speed drops to `4/5`, your time becomes `5/4`, which means you take `1/4` extra time! 

---

### Question 1
**Question:** Aeroplane covers distance at 240 kmph in 5 hours. To cover it in 5/3 hours, speed must be?
*   **Options:** `[A) 300  B) 360  C) 600  D) 720]`
*   **Mental Trick (Inverse Ratio):**
    *   Time drops from `5` to `5/3`. The time was divided by exactly **3**.
    *   Because Speed and Time are inversely proportional, if time is `/3`, the speed MUST be `*3`.
    *   `240 * 3 = 720`.
*   **Answer:** **D) 720 kmph**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Excluding stoppages, speed is 54 kmph. Including stoppages, it is 45 kmph. Halt per hour?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 10  C) 12  D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Stoppage Formula):**
    *   Halt Time = `(Difference in Speed) / (Original Faster Speed) * 60 minutes`.
    *   Difference = `54 - 45 = 9`.
    *   Time = `(9 / 54) * 60`.
    *   `9 / 54` simplifies clearly to `1/6`.
    *   `1/6 * 60 = 10 minutes`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 10**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Car travels first half distance at 40 km/hr and second half at 60 km/hr. Average speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 48  B) 37  C) 44  D) 45]`
*   **Mental Trick (Harmonic Mean):**
    *   When distances are EQUAL, average speed is NEVER simply `(a+b)/2`! It is `2ab / (a+b)`.
    *   `2 * 40 * 60 / (40 + 60)`.
    *   `4800 / 100 = 48`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 48 km/hr**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Walking at 5/7th of usual speed, you reach 16 minutes late. Usual time taken?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 40  C) 25  D) 30]`
*   **Mental Trick (Fraction Flip):**
    *   Speed becomes `5/7`.
    *   Time becomes `7/5`.
    *   Extra time taken = `7/5 - 1 = 2/5 of usual time`.
    *   This `2/5` represents the lateness!
    *   `2/5 of Usual = 16 minutes`.
    *   `Usual = 16 * 5 / 2 = 8 * 5 = 40 minutes`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 40 minutes**.

### Question 8
**Question:** At 3 kmph, I am 20 min late. At 4 kmph, I am 15 min early. Distance?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4 km  B) 5 km  C) 7 km  D) 6 km]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Super Formula):**
    *   `Distance = (Product of Speeds) / (Difference of Speeds) * (Total Time Gap)`.
    *   Time gap = `20 min late` to `15 min early` = `35 minutes`.
    *   Convert time to hours! `35/60`.
    *   `(3 * 4) / (4 - 3) * (35 / 60)`.
    *   `12 / 1 * 35 / 60`.
    *   `12/60` simplifies to `1/5`.
    *   `1/5 * 35 = 7 km`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 7 km**.

### Question 11
**Question:** Gun fired at 10.30 am. 2nd shot fired after 10m 30s. Bala hears it 10 mins after 1st shot. Speed of sound 330 m/s. Bala's speed in kmph?
*   **Options:** `[A) 19.8  B) 59.4  C) 58.6  D) 111.80]`
*   **Mental Trick (Sound and Car Ratio):**
    *   The sound of the 2nd bullet reached Bala 30 seconds "early" compared to the firing interval (10m 30s vs 10m 00s).
    *   Why? Because Bala's car drove towards the Sound source for exactly 10 minutes!
    *   `Distance traveled by Sound in 30 seconds = Distance traveled by Car in 10 minutes`.
    *   `Car Speed = Sound Speed * (Time of Sound / Time of Car)`.
    *   `Car Speed = 330 m/s * (30 sec / 600 sec)`.
    *   `Car Speed = 330 * (1/20) = 16.5 m/s`.
    *   Convert to kmph (multiply by 18/5!): `16.5 * 18/5 = 3.3 * 18 = 59.4`. (Mentally: 18*3=54, + 18*0.3=5.4 = 59.4!
*   **Answer:** **B) 59.4**.

### Question 13
**Question:** Cycles at 10 kmph -> arrives 1 pm. Cycles at 15 kmph -> arrives 11 am. Speed required to arrive exactly at noon (12 pm)?
*   **Options:** `[A) 11  B) 12  C) 13  D) 14]`
*   **Mental Trick (Distance Lock):**
    *   If speeds are 10 and 15, let's assume a common LCM distance: `30 km`.
    *   At 10 kmph, 30 km takes `3 hours`.
    *   At 15 kmph, 30 km takes `2 hours`.
    *   The time difference is `1 hour`. But the actual problem's difference is `2 hours` (1pm vs 11am).
    *   So, double the distance! Actual distance = **60 km**.
    *   At 10 kmph, 60 km takes 6 hours. Arriving at 1pm means he started at 7am!
    *   To arrive exactly at 12pm, he needs to cycle from 7am to 12pm (5 hours).
    *   Speed = `60 km / 5 hours = 12 kmph`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 12 kmph**.
