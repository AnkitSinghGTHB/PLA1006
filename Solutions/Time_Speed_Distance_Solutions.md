# Time, Speed and Distance - Mental Math Solutions

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for ALL 15 questions in the `TIME, SPEED AND DISTANCE.pdf` practice set.

### Question 1
**Question:** Aeroplane: 240 kmph, 5 hrs. Cover same distance in 5/3 hrs. Speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 300  B) 360  C) 600  D) 720]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Distance = 240 × 5 = 1200. Speed = 1200/(5/3) = 1200×3/5 = 720.
*   **Answer:** **D) 720 kmph**.

### Question 2
**Question:** Bus without stops: 54 kmph. With stops: 45 kmph. Stop time per hour?
*   **Options:** `[A) 9  B) 10  C) 12  D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Speed ratio = 45/54 = 5/6. Bus moves 5/6 of an hour = 50 min. Stops = 10 min.
*   **Answer:** **B) 10 minutes**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Half distance at 40, half at 60. Average speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 48  B) 37  C) 44  D) 45]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Harmonic mean for equal distances: 2×40×60/(40+60) = 4800/100 = 48.
*   **Answer:** **A) 48 km/hr**.

### Question 4
**Question:** At 14 instead of 10 kmph, walks 20 km more. Actual distance?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 56  C) 70  D) 80]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Same time T. 14T - 10T = 20 → 4T = 20 → T = 5. Distance = 10×5 = 50.
*   **Answer:** **A) 50 km**.

### Question 5
**Question:** Walking at 5/7 of usual speed, 16 min late. Usual time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 40  C) 25  D) 30]`
*   **Mental Trick:** At 5/7 speed, time = 7/5 of usual. Extra = 2/5 × T = 16 → T = 40.
*   **Answer:** **B) 40 minutes**.

### Question 6
**Question:** Front wheel circumference 40 ft, rear 48 ft. Front does 5 more revolutions. Distance?
*   **Options:** `[A) 950  B) 1450  C) 1200  D) 800]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let rear = n revolutions, front = n+5. 40(n+5) = 48n → 40n+200 = 48n → 8n = 200 → n = 25. Distance = 48×25 = 1200.
*   **Answer:** **C) 1200 ft**.

### Question 7
**Question:** Ram takes 3 hrs more than Karthik. Karthik is 5 kmph faster. Distance = 180 km. Ram's speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 8  B) 10  C) 15  D) 20]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Let Ram = s, Karthik = s+5. 180/s - 180/(s+5) = 3.
*   180(s+5-s)/s(s+5) = 3 → 900 = 3s(s+5) → s(s+5) = 300 → s²+5s-300 = 0.
*   (s+20)(s-15) = 0 → s = 15.
*   **Answer:** **C) 15 kmph**.

### Question 8
**Question:** At 3 kmph, 20 min late. At 4 kmph, 15 min early. Distance?
*   **Options:** `[A) 4  B) 5  C) 7  D) 6]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Time diff = 20+15 = 35 min = 7/12 hr. D/3 - D/4 = 7/12 → D/12 = 7/12 → D = 7.
*   **Answer:** **C) 7 km**.

### Question 9
**Question:** 100 kmph → 80 min. At 125 kmph, time?
*   **Options:** `[A) 64  B) 80  C) 60  D) 55]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Distance = 100×80/60 = 400/3 km. Time = (400/3)/125 × 60 = 400×60/(3×125) = 64 min.
*   **Answer:** **A) 64 minutes**.

### Question 10

*   **Mental Trick:** Time diff = 6 min = 1/10 hr. D/20 - D/25 = 1/10. D/100 = 1/10. D = 10 km.

### Question 11
**Question:** Kamal fires gun at 10:30. Second shot at 10:40:30 (10 min 30 sec later). Bala hears 2nd shot 10 min after 1st. Sound = 330 m/s. Bala's speed?
*   **Options:** `[A) 19.8  B) 59.4  C) 58.6  D) 111.80]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Time between shots = 630s. Time between hearings = 600s. Diff = 30s.
*   In 600s, Bala moved closer by: 330 × 30 = 9900m (sound covers this extra in 30s less).
*   Actually: 2nd sound travels 330×30 = 9900m less than 1st. Bala covered 9900m in 600s toward gun.
*   Speed = 9900/600 = 16.5 m/s = 59.4 kmph.
*   **Answer:** **B) 59.4 kmph**.

### Question 12
**Question:** Cycling at 8 kmph around square from A to C (diagonal opposite) in 30 min. Area?
*   **Options:** `[A) 64  B) 16  C) 9  D) 4]`
*   **Mental Trick:** A to C along boundary = 2 sides. Distance = 8×0.5 = 4 km. Each side = 2 km. Area = 4 km².
*   **Answer:** **D) 4 km²**.

### Question 13
**Question:** At 10 kmph → arrive 1 PM. At 15 kmph → arrive 11 AM. Speed for noon?
*   **Options:** `[A) 11  B) 12  C) 13  D) 14]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Time diff = 2 hrs. D/10 - D/15 = 2. D/30 = 2. D = 60 km.
*   Time at 10 kmph = 6 hrs → starts at 7 AM. Noon = 5 hrs after start. Speed = 60/5 = 12.
*   **Answer:** **B) 12 kmph**.

### Question 14
**Question:** At 5 kmph, 10 min late. At 7 kmph, 10 min early. Distance to station?
*   **Options:** `[A) 5.8  B) 35.6  C) 10.6  D) 92]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Diff = 20 min = 1/3 hr. D/5 - D/7 = 1/3. 2D/35 = 1/3. D = 35/6 ≈ 5.83.
*   **Answer:** **A) 5.8 km**.

### Question 15
**Question:** Travel Delhi-Gurgaon. After 30 km, speed becomes 4/5 of original, arrives 45 min late. After 48 km, same malfunction, 36 min late. Total distance?
*   **Options:** `[A) 100  B) 120  C) 150  D) 110]`
*   **Mental Trick:** Speed drop of 1/5 means each remaining km takes 1/4 extra time. Extra time ∝ remaining distance.
*   Case 1: remaining = D-30, extra = 45 min. Case 2: remaining = D-48, extra = 36 min.
*   (D-30)/(D-48) = 45/36 = 5/4. 4(D-30) = 5(D-48). 4D-120 = 5D-240. D = 120.
*   **Answer:** **B) 120 km**.
