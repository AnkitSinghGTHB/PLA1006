# Time and Work (Efficiencies) - Mental Math Solutions

[⬅️ Back to Main Navigation](./README.md) | [📖 Read Cheatsheet](./02_Time_Speed_Work_CheatSheet.md)

This document contains step-by-step mental math strategies for the `Time and Work -Working with different Efficiencies (1) (1).pdf` practice set.

> [!TIP]
> **The Efficiency-Time Flip:** Efficiency and Time are always inversely proportional! If A is twice as fast as B (2:1 efficiency), then A takes half the time of B (1:2 time). This relationship instantly solves "days less than" problems without algebra.

---

### Question 1
**Question:** Sachin & Dravid do work in 48 days. Sachin alone in 64 days. They worked 30 days, Sachin left. How long will Dravid take for the rest?
*   **Options:** `[A) 192  B) 72  C) 50  D) 13]`
*   **Mental Trick (LCM Method):**
    *   Work = `LCM(48, 64) = 192 units`. (Both divide nicely into 192).
    *   S+D eff = `192 / 48 = 4 units/day`.
    *   Sachin eff = `192 / 64 = 3 units/day`.
    *   Therefore, Dravid's efficiency = `4 - 3 = 1 unit/day`.
    *   They work together for 30 days: `30 days * 4 units = 120 units done`.
    *   Remaining work = `192 - 120 = 72 units`.
    *   Dravid alone takes: `72 / 1 = 72 days`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 72**.

### Question 3
**Question:** Cashier counts 100 notes in 1 minute. Machine does it in 20 seconds. Together how long for 100 notes?
*   **Options:** `[A) 15 s  B) 16 s  C) 14 s  D) 12 s]`
*   **Mental Trick (Rate per Second):**
    *   A "bundle of 100 notes" is just **1 unit of work**.
    *   Cashier = 1 unit per 60 seconds `(1/60)`.
    *   Machine = 1 unit per 20 seconds `(1/20)`.
    *   Together = `1/60 + 1/20`. Make denominators equal: `1/60 + 3/60 = 4/60`.
    *   Simplify `4/60` = `1/15`.
    *   They do 1/15th of the bundle per second, so they finish in 15 seconds!
*   **Answer:** **A) 15 s**.

### Question 4
**Question:** Ram is twice as efficient as Sunita and finishes in 25 days less. Sunita finishes in?
*   **Options:** `[A) 50  B) 10  C) 20  D) 27]`
*   **Mental Trick (The Flip):**
    *   Efficiency Ratio (Ram : Sunita) = `2 : 1`.
    *   Time Ratio (Ram : Sunita) = **`1 : 2`**.
    *   Difference in parts = `2 - 1 = 1 part`.
    *   Problem says difference is 25 days! So, `1 part = 25 days`.
    *   Sunita takes 2 parts. `2 * 25 = 50 days`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 50**.

### Question 5
**Question:** A in 12 days, B in 18. Alternate days starting with A. How many days to finish?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10.8  B) 14.33  C) 11  D) 8.4]`
*   **Mental Trick (Cycle Method):**
    *   Work = `LCM(12, 18) = 36 units`.
    *   A eff = `3`, B eff = `2`.
    *   They work in 2-day cycles (A then B). One 2-day cycle = `3 + 2 = 5 units`.
    *   How many full 5-unit cycles fit into 36? `36 / 5 = 7 cycles` with 1 unit leftover.
    *   `7 cycles` = **14 days**.
    *   On the 15th day, it is A's turn again. A needs to do 1 unit, but his capacity is 3 units per day.
    *   Time taken = `1/3 of a day`.
    *   Total time = `14 + 1/3 = 14.33 days`.
*   **Answer:** **B) 14.33 days**.

### Question 8
**Question:** A completes 4 hours lesser than B. Together they take 288 minutes. B alone takes?
*   **Options:** `[A) 1 hr  B) 2 hr  C) 3 hr  D) 12 hr]`
*   **Mental Trick (Option Testing):**
    *   `288 minutes` = `288 / 60 = 4.8 hours`.
    *   Test Option D (12 hr) for B.
    *   If B = 12 hr, then A = 8 hr (since A is 4 hrs less).
    *   Let's check their combined time using the product/sum formula: `(A * B) / (A + B)`.
    *   `(12 * 8) / (12 + 8) = 96 / 20 = 4.8 hours`.
    *   It matches perfectly instantly!
*   **Answer:** **D) 12 hr**.

### Question 9
**Question:** X does work in 20 days. Worked for 5 days, Y finished the rest in 15 days. X and Y together finish in?
*   **Options:** `[A) 10  B) 15  C) 18  D) 17]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   X worked for 5 days. Since X's total time is 20 days, X completed `5/20 = 1/4` of the work.
    *   Remaining work = `3/4`.
    *   Y completed this `3/4` work in 15 days.
    *   Y's total time for 100% work = `15 * (4/3) = 20 days`.
    *   Since X takes 20 days and Y ALSO takes 20 days, they are identically efficient!
    *   Two identical workers will halve the time: `20 / 2 = 10 days`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 10**.

### Question 13
**Question:** Chetan is thrice as efficient as Seeta. Together 60 days. Seeta alone?
*   **Options:** `[A) 80  B) 200  C) 240  D) 24]`
*   **Mental Trick (Total Work Transfer):**
    *   Chetan eff = 3, Seeta eff = 1.
    *   Combined eff = 4.
    *   They worked for 60 days with an eff of 4. Total Work = `60 * 4 = 240 units`.
    *   If Seeta does it alone, she uses her eff of 1.
    *   `240 / 1 = 240 days`.
*   **Answer:** **C) 240**.

### Question 15
**Question:** A is twice as good as B, finishes 30 days less. Working together?
*   **Options:** `[A) 20  B) 12  C) 30  D) 23]`
*   **Mental Trick:**
    *   Efficiency A:B = `2:1`. So Time A:B = `1:2`.
    *   Time difference = `2 - 1 = 1 part`.
    *   `1 part = 30 days`.
    *   So A takes 30 days, B takes 60 days.
    *   Together = `(30 * 60) / (30 + 60) = 1800 / 90 = 20 days`.
*   **Answer:** **A) 20**.
